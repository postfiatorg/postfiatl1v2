## Activities

Permanent Upper Class publicly presents itself as a builder of software and data systems involving AI-assisted market intelligence, trading research, trend corpora, agent-payment and accountability tools, and Post Fiat infrastructure. Its GitHub organization identifies public repositories for a validator dashboard, network analytics, trading intelligence, contributor tools, and agent-economy services. [GitHub organization](https://github.com/P-U-C)

Its `b1e55ed` product is described as an open-source, event-sourced trading-intelligence system for operators, software builders, AI agents, and trading bots; the public site states that execution remains in paper-trading mode. [b1e55ed product site](https://b1e55ed.permanentupperclass.com/)

Within Post Fiat, it publishes Lens, an on-chain network-intelligence dashboard, and SUBS, an on-chain service-subscription protocol. These products address Post Fiat contributors, service providers, and service users. [Lens](https://pft.permanentupperclass.com/lens/)

Its relationship to the specified validator is that of the self-declared operator and publisher of the corresponding domain-verification materials. [Validator page](https://pft.permanentupperclass.com/) [Validator repository](https://github.com/P-U-C/pft-validator)

Permanent Upper Class is the supported public name of a pseudonymous software and blockchain-infrastructure operator; no corresponding legal entity or incorporation jurisdiction has been established. Its principal operating base is also not established, and its public materials present it as an internet-based coordination layer rather than identifying a headquarters.
