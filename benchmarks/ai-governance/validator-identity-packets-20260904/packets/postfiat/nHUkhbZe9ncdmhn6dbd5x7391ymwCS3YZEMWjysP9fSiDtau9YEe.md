# Organization Profile

## Identity

The canonical public name is **Post Fiat**. Its official materials describe it as an XRP-derived Layer 1 blockchain project and network; a LinkedIn organization page identifies it as an association, but no incorporated legal entity or legal name was established. No aliases or former names are sufficiently supported; “PFTL” refers to the protocol, while AGTI appears to be a related development organization rather than an alias. [Post Fiat’s official About page](https://postfiat.org/about/) identifies the project and founder, and its [LinkedIn organization page](https://pr.linkedin.com/company/post-fiat) describes its organizational type.

The project’s [LinkedIn organization page](https://pr.linkedin.com/company/post-fiat) identifies San Juan as its headquarters and principal location, while the founder’s [LinkedIn profile](https://pr.linkedin.com/in/alex-good-90259a14) associates his Post Fiat role with San Juan. No additional principal operating regions were established.

## Activities

Post Fiat develops an XRP-derived Layer 1 public testnet oriented toward capital markets and collective intelligence. Its stated work includes auditable validator-selection and scoring infrastructure, privacy-enabled financial workflows, indexing, research coordination, compliance-oriented tooling, and AI-assisted capital workflows. Its public products include Task Node, PFTerminal, validator setup and scoring tools, a network explorer, and developer repositories. Its stated stakeholders include validator operators, developers, contributors, capital-markets researchers, buy-side users, compliance users, and participants in AI-routed workflows. [Official homepage](https://postfiat.org/), [About page](https://postfiat.org/about/), and [PFTerminal product page](https://postfiat.org/terminal/).

The validator relationship is supported by the exact key-to-domain mapping in the [public validator registry](https://mikeyexplains.com/webpage_network_health_and_validator_board.php), combined with the domain’s identification as the Post Fiat project site. Specific ownership or operational control of the key remains unestablished.

## Uncertainty

No government filing, regulator record, or official legal page establishing Post Fiat’s incorporated legal name, jurisdiction, ownership, or legal form was found. The LinkedIn “association” designation is therefore treated as a self-description, not proof of incorporation.

The validator registry associates the supplied key with `postfiat.org`, but several validator keys claim the same domain. The available evidence does not identify the individual or legal entity controlling this particular key. Server-location and domain-registration geography were excluded from the operating-base analysis.

AGTI was not treated as an alias, parent, or legal owner of Post Fiat: public materials describe it as a related capital and development engine, but the precise legal and ownership relationship is not established. “PFTL” was treated as a protocol abbreviation rather than an organizational alias.
