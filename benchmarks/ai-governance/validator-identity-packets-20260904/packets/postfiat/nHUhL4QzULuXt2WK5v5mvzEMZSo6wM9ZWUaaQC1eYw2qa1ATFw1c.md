# Organization Profile

## Identity

**Canonical public name:** Preaware Labs, styled **PRE/AWARE LABS**. Supported aliases are **PRE/AWARE** and **Preaware**. No former name is established.

**Entity type:** Individual-run public research and open-source project; no separate company, foundation, nonprofit, or other incorporated entity is established. The official site states that “Preaware is run by Viktor Vecsei,” and a project article is signed “Viktor / Preaware.” [Preaware homepage](https://preaware.org/) [Terma project article](https://preaware.org/posts/terma/)

**Validator connection:** The official homepage identifies `POSTFIAT_VALIDATOR` as an active project and displays the matching abbreviated public key `nHUhL4Qz...2qa1ATFw1c`. This matches the supplied full key at both ends and directly links the claimed domain to the validator. [Preaware homepage](https://preaware.org/) Post Fiat’s official setup guide explains that a validator domain is bound through a published domain attestation containing its public key. [Post Fiat validator setup guide](https://postfiat.org/validator-setup/)

Viktor Vecsei’s linked GitHub profile identifies him as IVPN’s COO, while IVPN’s official team page says he leads its operations; these sources corroborate the operator’s public identity but do **not** establish IVPN as owner or operator of Preaware. [GitHub profile](https://github.com/johnnyburnaway) [IVPN team page](https://cdn.ivpn.net/en/team/)

## Activities

Preaware publicly frames its work around privacy, service operations, sovereignty, research and education, autonomous agents, and open source. Its listed projects include a Post Fiat validator, the Terma research agent, a Post Fiat weekly chronicle, and a curated privacy-preserving LLM-tools directory. [Preaware homepage](https://preaware.org/)

Terma provides sourced answers and research reports about Post Fiat through Task Node, using a corpus of more than 60 indexed sources. Its described users are Post Fiat participants seeking answers about the network. [Terma project article](https://preaware.org/posts/terma/) The LLM Privacy Stack is an open-source directory aimed at privacy-conscious users and builders evaluating local inference, private cloud inference, routers, and related tools. [LLM Privacy Stack repository](https://github.com/johnnyburnaway/llm-privacy-stack)

The validator relationship is presented as one component of this broader personal research and open-source portfolio. The available evidence supports public attribution to Preaware and Viktor Vecsei, but it does not establish a separate corporate operator.
