## Incorporation and Operations

**Incorporation jurisdiction:** **Not established — low confidence.** No accessible corporate filing, regulator record, or official legal page was found that identifies a registered Post Fiat entity or incorporation jurisdiction. The project’s use of “foundation” in governance descriptions is not sufficient evidence of an incorporated foundation. ([Post Fiat whitepaper](https://postfiat.org/whitepaper/))

**Principal operating region:** **San Juan, Puerto Rico — moderate confidence.** The project’s LinkedIn page lists San Juan as both headquarters and principal location. This is evidence of a stated operating base, not incorporation. ([Post Fiat LinkedIn](https://pr.linkedin.com/company/post-fiat))

**Geographic reach:** International-facing and online, but specific staffed regions beyond Puerto Rico are not established. The project publicly serves network operators, builders, contributors, users, and capital-markets participants through web applications, documentation, repositories, and a public testnet. ([About Post Fiat](https://postfiat.org/about/), [Post Fiat GitHub](https://github.com/postfiatorg))

## Activities

Post Fiat develops an XRP-derived Layer 1 and associated software for settlement, validator governance, privacy-enabled workflows, and AI-assisted financial coordination. Its public surfaces include a testnet, explorer, validator-selection research and tooling, Task Node contributor workflows, PFTerminal, indexing infrastructure, encrypted-document and market-intelligence applications, and NAVCoin-related research and implementations. ([Post Fiat homepage](https://postfiat.org/), [About Post Fiat](https://postfiat.org/about/), [Post Fiat GitHub](https://github.com/postfiatorg))

The intended stakeholders include validators, contributors, builders, users and integrators, researchers, asset owners, portfolio managers, expert networks, and compliance-oriented capital-markets participants. ([Post Fiat whitepaper](https://postfiat.org/whitepaper/))

The validator relationship is supported at the domain-identity level: the exact public key is reported with `postfiat.org`, while the domain is Post Fiat’s official web identity. This does not independently establish which person or legal entity has custody of the validator keys. ([Validator-key/domain record](https://mikeyexplains.com/webpage_network_health_and_validator_board.php), [Post Fiat validator setup](https://postfiat.org/validator-setup/))

## Business Summary

Post Fiat is the supported public name of a small, founder-led blockchain project and network; a separately incorporated legal entity was not established. Its self-published LinkedIn page describes it as an association headquartered in San Juan, Puerto Rico, while no filing establishes its incorporation jurisdiction. The project develops an XRP-derived Layer 1, validator-selection and governance tooling, privacy-enabled settlement workflows, Task Node contributor coordination, PFTerminal, indexing, and NAVCoin-related infrastructure. Its intended stakeholders include validators, software builders, Task Node contributors, users and integrators, and capital-markets participants such as asset owners, portfolio managers, researchers, and compliance-oriented teams. The organization publishes software, research, a testnet explorer, and participation tools online, giving it an international-facing reach even though its only stated operating base is Puerto Rico. Its public footprint is Micro, based on a self-reported 2–10 employee range; exact headcount is not established.

## Uncertainty

- No legal entity name, incorporation record, jurisdiction, registration number, or nonprofit status was established.
- “Post Fiat Foundation” or generic references to “the foundation” were excluded as canonical names because the available materials use them functionally and do not identify a registered foundation.
- San Juan, Puerto Rico is a self-reported headquarters and operating location, not evidence of incorporation.
- Exact employee headcount, employment relationships, ownership, and governance personnel beyond the named founder are not established.
- The exact validator-key/domain pairing is visible through a secondary public network-data source. The domain’s own attestation endpoint could not be accessed during research, so the attestation contents were not independently inspected.
- The evidence supports a connection between the validator and Post Fiat’s official domain, but it does not prove which individual or legal entity controls the validator’s private keys.
- `@postfiatorg` is linked by the official GitHub organization, but the X profile itself returned an access restriction and was not inspected directly.
