## Activities

Wizbubba publicly operates a Post Fiat testnet validator and maintains a domain-based identity, proof-link, status, and change-log surface for it ([validator homepage](https://pft.wizbubba.xyz/), [change log](https://pft.wizbubba.xyz/change-log/)).

The same public identity authors the **Journal Node Audit Suite**, described in its self-published specification as an LLM-readability audit product line for validator websites and broader crypto-project materials. The described output includes scored public GitHub Gists and prioritized improvements, with intended stakeholders including crypto-project teams, builders, research-bot operators, project-discovery agents, and AI-mediated capital allocators ([canonical audit specification](https://gist.github.com/journalnode/0fd802b1791ee736953b49ceb6363399)). These are stated target users; commercial adoption and paying customers are not established.

The validator connection is direct at the domain-claim level: the homepage and `validator.json` bind Wizbubba, the claimed domain, and the supplied public key in the same records ([homepage](https://pft.wizbubba.xyz/), [validator.json](https://pft.wizbubba.xyz/validator.json)). The domain-attestation file and explorer details page were not successfully retrievable during this research, so independent network-side confirmation was not completed.
