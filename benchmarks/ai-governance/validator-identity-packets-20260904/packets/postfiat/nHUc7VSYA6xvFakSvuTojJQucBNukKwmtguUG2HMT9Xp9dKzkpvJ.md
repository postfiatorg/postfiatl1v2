# Organization Profile

## Identity

The most supportable canonical public name is **Post Fiat**. It presents itself as an XRP-derived blockchain network and open-source project for capital markets and collective intelligence; no corresponding legal entity name is disclosed on its official About page or machine-readable project record. ([About Post Fiat](https://postfiat.org/about/), [project record](https://postfiat.org/postfiat-project.json))

**Entity type:** Open-source blockchain network/project; legal corporate, nonprofit, or foundation status is **Not established**.

**Aliases or former names:** None established. “PFT” is identified as the network’s native asset, not an organizational alias. “AGTI” is separately described as a development corporation associated with Post Fiat and should not be treated as an alias or proven legal parent. ([About Post Fiat](https://postfiat.org/about/), [founder’s description of AGTI](https://goodalexander.com/posts/postfiat_day13/))

**Validator connection:** A public validator directory pairs the exact key `nHUc7VSYA6xvFakSvuTojJQucBNukKwmtguUG2HMT9Xp9dKzkpvJ` with `postfiat.org`. The domain resolves to the official Post Fiat project site, while Post Fiat’s validator documentation specifies domain-controlled `pft-ledger.toml` attestations as its key-to-domain proof mechanism. This supports an association with the Post Fiat project, but not the identity of a separately incorporated operator. ([key–domain directory entry](https://mikeyexplains.com/webpage_network_health_and_validator_board.php), [Post Fiat validator-attestation procedure](https://postfiat.org/validator-setup/))

## Activities

Post Fiat develops and publishes an XRP-derived public-testnet protocol, validator-selection research and tooling, privacy-enabled financial-workflow designs, and open-source ledger, wallet, explorer, and developer components. Its official product surfaces include Task Node, PFTerminal, validator setup and scoring tools, indexing methods, encrypted-document workflows, market-intelligence reports, and wallet or chatbot interfaces. Intended stakeholders include validators, software contributors, researchers, and users working in buy-side research, indexing, compliance, expert networks, and AI-assisted capital deployment. ([official homepage](https://postfiat.org/), [About Post Fiat](https://postfiat.org/about/), [official GitHub organization](https://github.com/postfiatorg))

The validator relationship is supported at the public-identity level by the exact key–domain pairing and by the project’s documented domain-attestation system. The available evidence does not distinguish whether the node is operated directly by a legal entity, by a founder, or by another operator authorized to use the project domain. ([validator directory](https://mikeyexplains.com/webpage_network_health_and_validator_board.php), [validator setup documentation](https://postfiat.org/validator-setup/))

## Business Summary

Post Fiat is the public name of an open-source, XRP-derived blockchain-network project focused on capital markets and collective intelligence. No legal entity bearing that name, incorporation jurisdiction, or principal operating base was established from the accessible primary sources. The project publishes public-testnet infrastructure, validator-selection research and tooling, privacy-enabled settlement designs, the Task Node contributor platform, PFTerminal, indexing methods, and related developer software. Its intended stakeholders include validator operators, software contributors, researchers, and users of AI-assisted capital-markets, compliance, expert-network, and financial-coordination workflows. Its public reach is internet-based and potentially global, but no formal geographic operating footprint is disclosed. The public institutional footprint is classified as Micro: one founder is named, the official GitHub organization maintains multiple repositories, and the website displays several pseudonymous community contributors, while employee headcount and the legal relationship between Post Fiat and the separately described AGTI development corporation remain unestablished.

## Uncertainty

- No official Post Fiat legal page, regulator filing, or company-registry record reviewed identifies a legal entity named “Post Fiat,” its incorporation jurisdiction, ownership, or principal office.
- The direct contents of `https://postfiat.org/.well-known/pft-ledger.toml` were not accessible through the browsing interface. Consequently, the full domain-attestation chain for the supplied key could not be independently inspected.
- The third-party directory containing the exact key–domain pair describes itself as an XRP Ledger validator map, while the supplied context identifies the key with the Post Fiat network. The mapping supports the domain association but leaves the precise network history or reuse of the key unresolved.
- AGTI is associated with Post Fiat in project-authored material, but no reviewed source establishes that AGTI owns `postfiat.org`, controls the supplied validator key, or is Post Fiat’s legal parent. It is therefore excluded as an alias and legal identity.
- Employee headcount, ownership, funding, revenue, and formal governance are not established. Community contributors and repository contributors were not counted as employees.
- The official Post Fiat page names Alex Good as founder, while some third-party material describes Alex Good and Travis Good as co-founders; no second founder was confirmed from the reviewed official Post Fiat identity page.
