# Organization Profile

## Identity

**Canonical public name:** Bithomp.

**Entity type:** A commercial blockchain-data and software organization. Its legal structure is not completely resolved publicly: the official site identifies **Bithomp AB**, a Swedish limited company, in its footer, while its current terms define **Ledger Explorer Ltd.** as the company providing the service and state that Ledger Explorer owns the Bithomp brand ([About Bithomp](https://bithomp.com/about-us), [Terms and Conditions](https://bithomp.com/terms-and-conditions)). These are separate company names, not established aliases or former names.

The validator connection is strong. Bithomp’s domain-hosted XRPL metadata names “Bithomp” as principal and lists the exact mainnet validator key `nHB8QMKGt9VB4Vg71VszjBVQnDW3v3QudM4DwFaJfy96bj4Pv9fA` ([bithomp.com XRPL TOML](https://bithomp.com/.well-known/xrp-ledger.toml)). This establishes control of the domain-side validator claim, but not which of the two disclosed legal entities holds the validator credentials.

**Identity confidence:** High for the Bithomp organization; medium for the precise legal entity operating the validator.

## Official Web Presence

- **Official website:** [https://bithomp.com](https://bithomp.com). The site describes Bithomp’s products, names its associated companies, and hosts the validator declaration ([About Bithomp](https://bithomp.com/about-us), [XRPL TOML](https://bithomp.com/.well-known/xrp-ledger.toml)).
- **Official X handle:** [@bithomp](https://x.com/bithomp). The basis is the `x = "bithomp"` declaration in Bithomp’s own domain-hosted XRPL metadata; the X profile itself was not independently readable during this research ([XRPL TOML](https://bithomp.com/.well-known/xrp-ledger.toml)).

## Incorporation and Operations

- **Incorporation jurisdiction:** **Sweden for Bithomp AB — high confidence.** Bithomp’s site publishes Swedish organization number `559342-2867`; Swedish company-data records report that Bithomp AB was registered in 2021 as an *aktiebolag* with its seat in Stockholm ([About Bithomp](https://bithomp.com/about-us), [Bithomp AB company record](https://www.bolagsfakta.se/5593422867-Bithomp_AB)).  
  **Ledger Explorer Ltd.: Not established.** Bithomp’s legal pages give it a Victoria, Mahé, Seychelles address, but no registration number or independently verified registry extract was found ([Terms and Conditions](https://bithomp.com/terms-and-conditions), [Privacy Policy](https://bithomp.com/privacy-policy)).
- **Principal operating region:** **Stockholm, Sweden — medium confidence.** Bithomp AB’s organization-maintained LinkedIn page identifies Stockholm as its headquarters, while the domain metadata identifies the validator owner country as Sweden ([Bithomp AB on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/bithomp), [XRPL TOML](https://bithomp.com/.well-known/xrp-ledger.toml)). The Seychelles address appears to be a legal or administrative address; operational activity there is not established.
- **Geographic reach:** Internet-based, serving users and developers of XRPL and Xahau. No additional physical operating regions are established ([About Bithomp](https://bithomp.com/about-us)).

## Activities

Bithomp provides browser-based explorers for XRP Ledger and Xahau, account and transaction searches, token and NFT discovery, ledger statistics, test-network explorers and faucets, and paid or free API access for developers and other data users ([About Bithomp](https://bithomp.com/about-us), [Bithomp API documentation](https://docs.bithomp.com/)). Its stated stakeholders include individual ledger users, software developers, API customers, and participants interacting with tokens and NFTs.

Its relationship to the validator is direct at the public-identity level: the official `bithomp.com` XRPL TOML identifies Bithomp as principal and publishes the supplied validator key as a mainnet validator ([XRPL TOML](https://bithomp.com/.well-known/xrp-ledger.toml)).

## Public Footprint

**Micro (about 1–10 people).** Swedish company data reports two employees for Bithomp AB in its 2024 figures, while the organization-maintained LinkedIn page describes a 2–10-person company and displays three associated employees ([Bithomp AB company record](https://www.bolagsfakta.se/5593422867-Bithomp_AB), [Bithomp AB on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/bithomp)). These sources consistently indicate a very small organization, although they do not establish a consolidated current headcount across Bithomp AB and Ledger Explorer Ltd.

**Confidence:** Medium-high. **Current consolidated headcount:** Not established.

## Business Summary

Bithomp is the supported public name of a privately operated blockchain-data and software organization. Its website identifies the Swedish limited company Bithomp AB and also names Seychelles-based Ledger Explorer Ltd as the company governing the service, so the exact legal entity controlling the validator is not conclusively established. The strongest supported incorporation record is Bithomp AB in Sweden, with Stockholm as its stated operating base; Ledger Explorer Ltd is presented at a Victoria, Mahé address, but its incorporation was not independently confirmed. Bithomp provides web explorers, APIs, NFT discovery and transaction tools, ledger statistics, and test-network tools for XRP Ledger and Xahau users and developers. Its public reach is internet-based and serves blockchain users, software builders, and API customers. The organization falls in the Micro size tier, with public sources indicating only a few personnel.

## Uncertainty

- The exact legal entity controlling the validator’s private credentials is not established. The domain links the validator to Bithomp, but does not name Bithomp AB or Ledger Explorer Ltd.
- The ownership, control, or contractual relationship between Bithomp AB and Ledger Explorer Ltd. is not disclosed by the sources reviewed.
- Ledger Explorer Ltd.’s Seychelles incorporation was not independently confirmed through a registry extract; only its official-site legal address was established.
- Bithomp’s terms identify the defined “Country” as Malta while giving the company a Seychelles address. Malta was therefore excluded as an incorporation or operating jurisdiction.
- The official pages vary between “Royal street” and “Revolution Avenue” for Ledger Explorer Ltd.’s address.
- No supported former names were found. “XRPL Explorer” and “XRP Explorer” were treated as product descriptions rather than aliases.
- Current consolidated headcount across both disclosed companies is not established.
