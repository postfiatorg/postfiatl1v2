# Organization Profile

## Identity

The organization is the **University of Nicosia**, commonly abbreviated **UNIC**, a private, for-profit university and corporate legal entity in Cyprus. Its charter gives “University of Nicosia” as its name, describes it as a corporate legal entity, and identifies Nicosia as its headquarters ([University Charter](https://www.unic.ac.cy/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/UNIC-CHARTER-4.pdf)). Cyprus government sources independently classify it as a private university and private for-profit organization ([Department of Higher Education](https://highereducation.ac.cy/index.php/en/citizens-info-serv-he-institutions-en), [Commission for the Protection of Competition](https://www.competition.gov.cy/competition/competition.nsf/All/B19A01B8151E202DC2258AC5002F0569?OpenDocument=&print=)).

Supported alias: **UNIC** ([UNIC at a Glance](https://www.unic.ac.cy/unic-at-a-glance/)). No former legal name is established.

The validator connection is supported by two complementary records: the university says that it hosts and maintains an XRPL validator, while a public XRPL registry pairs `xrp.unic.ac.cy` with public key `nHUfPizyJyhAJZzeq3duRVrZmsTZfcLn7yLF5s2adzHdcHMb9HmQ` ([UNIC XRPL Grants](https://www.unic.ac.cy/el/iff/research/funded-research/xrpl-grants/), [Bithomp validator registry](https://bithomp.com/validators?amendment=fixXChainRewardRounding)). Identity confidence: **High**.

## Official Web Presence

Official website: [https://www.unic.ac.cy/](https://www.unic.ac.cy/). The Cyprus Department of Higher Education identifies this as the university’s website ([government institution record](https://highereducation.ac.cy/index.php/en/idrymata/panepistimio-lefkosias-en)).

Official X handle: [@UNIC_ENG](https://x.com/UNIC_ENG). Official university publications identify `UNIC_ENG` as its X/Twitter account ([University Guide 2022–2023](https://www.unic.ac.cy/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/UNIC-GUIDE-2022-2023-EN-1.pdf)).

## Incorporation and Operations

**Incorporation/constitution jurisdiction — Republic of Cyprus; high confidence.** The university’s charter establishes it as a corporate legal entity headquartered in Nicosia, while the Cyprus government records that the University of Nicosia was registered and licensed under the country’s private-university legislation ([University Charter](https://www.unic.ac.cy/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/UNIC-CHARTER-4.pdf), [Cyprus higher-education institutions](https://highereducation.ac.cy/index.php/en/citizens-info-serv-he-institutions-en)).

**Principal operating regions — Cyprus and Greece; high confidence.** Nicosia, Cyprus, is described as the university’s headquarters and home base. It also operates UNIC Athens in Elliniko, Greece, and supplies distance-learning programmes internationally ([UNIC at a Glance](https://www.unic.ac.cy/unic-at-a-glance/), [official university homepage](https://www.unic.ac.cy/), [Study at UNIC](https://www.unic.ac.cy/study-at-unic/)).

## Activities

The University of Nicosia provides undergraduate, master’s, doctoral, professional, on-campus, and distance-learning education across eight schools and more than 100 programmes. It also conducts basic, applied, and interdisciplinary research and collaborates with research centres, industry, professional bodies, and society ([UNIC at a Glance](https://www.unic.ac.cy/unic-at-a-glance/), [Study at UNIC](https://www.unic.ac.cy/study-at-unic/)).

Its blockchain activities include teaching, research, scholarships, technical development, hackathons, and participation in Ripple’s University Blockchain Research Initiative. The university states that grant funding supports establishment and maintenance of an XRPL validator used for research and project-based learning ([University Blockchain Research Initiative](https://www.unic.ac.cy/iff/research/funded-research/university-blockchain-research-initiative/)).

## Public Footprint

**Very large.** The university reports more than 11,500 students from over 100 countries, over 100 programmes, eight schools, campuses in Nicosia and Athens, and international distance-learning operations ([UNIC at a Glance](https://www.unic.ac.cy/unic-at-a-glance/), [official university homepage](https://www.unic.ac.cy/)). Confidence: **High**. A current independently verified employee headcount is **not established**; the tier reflects the scale and geographic reach of the public institution.

## Business Summary

University of Nicosia (UNIC) is a private, for-profit university and corporate legal entity constituted in the Republic of Cyprus. Its principal operating base is Nicosia, Cyprus, with an additional campus in Athens, Greece, and distance-learning services reaching students internationally. The university provides undergraduate, master’s, doctoral, and professional education; conducts basic, applied, and interdisciplinary research; and works with students, researchers, industry, professional bodies, and society. Its portfolio includes more than 100 on-campus and online programmes across eight schools, including medicine, health sciences, business, law, engineering, and blockchain-related disciplines. It serves more than 11,500 students from over 100 countries. Its public footprint is therefore classified as Very large, although a current, independently verified employee headcount is not established.

## Uncertainty

The validator’s TOML file at `xrp.unic.ac.cy` was not accessible during research, so the exact key-to-domain pairing relies on the public Bithomp registry rather than direct inspection of that file. The registry also displays the personal name “Andreas Pavlou,” but no primary source establishes whether this person owns or controls the validator independently or administers it for the university; the name is therefore excluded from the organization’s identity and aliases.

The university’s historical relationship with predecessor institution Intercollege does not establish that “Intercollege” was a former legal name of the University of Nicosia, so it is not treated as an alias. A current independently verified employee headcount was not established.
