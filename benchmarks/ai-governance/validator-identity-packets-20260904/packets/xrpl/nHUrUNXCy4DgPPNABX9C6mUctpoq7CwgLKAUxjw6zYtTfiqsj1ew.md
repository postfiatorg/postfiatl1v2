# Organization Profile

## Identity

The canonical public and legal name is **Interledger Foundation**, a nonprofit private operating foundation incorporated in California. Its supported abbreviation is **ILF**; no former names are established. [Official Foundation materials](https://interledger.org/grant/grantmaking-faq/) describe its entity type, while its [About page](https://interledger.org/about-us) uses “ILF.”

The validator identity is strongly supported. Bithomp maps the exact public key `nHUrUNXCy4DgPPNABX9C6mUctpoq7CwgLKAUxjw6zYtTfiqsj1ew` to `xrp-validator.interledger.org` and reports a TOML-verified domain relationship. [Official XRP Ledger documentation](https://xrpl.org/docs/references/xrp-ledger-toml) explains that this two-way verification provides strong evidence that the same entity controls the domain and validator. Interledger Foundation’s [privacy policy](https://interledger.org/privacy-policy) identifies “Interledger” as the Foundation and describes its websites and subdomains as part of its owned and operated community. Together, these sources support attribution to Interledger Foundation, although no separately retrieved Foundation-authored statement explicitly names this validator.

## Activities

Interledger Foundation develops, maintains, and governs open payment technologies, notably the Interledger Protocol, Open Payments API, and Web Monetization standard. It also makes grants supporting technical integration, research, education, policy work, open-source development, and digital-financial-inclusion initiatives. Its stakeholders include financial-service providers, fintechs, cooperatives, universities, developers, civil-society organizations, regulators, policymakers, and financially underserved communities. The Foundation states that it is not a financial institution and does not itself operate a payment network. [Grantmaking FAQ](https://interledger.org/grant/grantmaking-faq/) [Frequently Asked Questions](https://interledger.org/faq/)

The verified domain-key relationship supports that the Foundation is connected to this XRP Ledger validator. The public sources checked do not explain the validator’s organizational purpose, governance arrangements, or how it relates operationally to the Foundation’s primarily non-blockchain open-payments programs. ## Business Summary

Interledger Foundation is a California-incorporated nonprofit private operating foundation that stewards open payment standards and supports digital financial inclusion. It principally operates through a globally distributed team reported across 17 countries; no single current operating headquarters is established in the sources reviewed. Its work includes developing and governing the Interledger Protocol, Open Payments API and Web Monetization standard, building related open infrastructure, making grants, supporting technical implementers, and engaging regulators and standards bodies. Its stakeholders include financial-service providers, fintechs, cooperatives, universities, open-source developers, civil-society organizations, policymakers, and underserved communities seeking access to digital financial services. The foundation reports programs and technology activity spanning multiple countries. Its public institutional footprint is Medium, based on an official report of more than 50 team members and a substantial global grantmaking and project portfolio, although an exact post-restructuring headcount is not established.
