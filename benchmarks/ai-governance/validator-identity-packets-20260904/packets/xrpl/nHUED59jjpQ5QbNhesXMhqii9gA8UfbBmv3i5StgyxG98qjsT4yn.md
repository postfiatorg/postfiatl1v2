# Organization Profile

## Identity

**Canonical name:** Blockdaemon Inc.  
**Entity type:** Privately held Delaware corporation providing blockchain-infrastructure software and services. “Blockdaemon” is the supported public/brand name; no former name is established. The company’s privacy policy identifies the legal entity as Blockdaemon Inc., while its LEI record identifies it as an active Delaware corporation ([Blockdaemon Privacy Policy](https://www.blockdaemon.com/privacy-policy), [Bloomberg LEI record](https://lei.bloomberg.com/leis/view/254900RSXBG1LL701F39)).

The best-supported attribution of the validator is **Blockdaemon Inc.** Bithomp maps public key `nHUED59jjpQ5QbNhesXMhqii9gA8UfbBmv3i5StgyxG98qjsT4yn` to the TOML-verified domain `arrington-xrp-capital.blockdaemon.com` and associates it with “Support Team, Konstantin Richter”; Blockdaemon’s official site identifies Richter as its founder and CEO ([Bithomp validator registry](https://bithomp.com/validators?amendment=fixXChainRewardRounding), [Blockdaemon About](https://www.blockdaemon.com/about)). A historical validator table also labels that domain’s entity as Blockdaemon ([July 2021 validator table](https://www.crypto-law.us/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/ex6_7_8.pdf)).

The subdomain label mentions Arrington XRP Capital, but that string alone does not establish that Arrington owns or controls the validator.

## Official Web Presence

- **Official website:** [https://www.blockdaemon.com/](https://www.blockdaemon.com/) — identified as Blockdaemon’s website by its corporate privacy policy and LEI record ([Privacy Policy](https://www.blockdaemon.com/privacy-policy), [LEI record](https://lei.bloomberg.com/leis/view/254900RSXBG1LL701F39)).
- **Official X handle:** [@BlockdaemonHQ](https://x.com/BlockdaemonHQ) — the profile identifies itself as Blockdaemon and links to `blockdaemon.com`; an official Blockdaemon-hosted publication also lists this handle ([X profile](https://x.com/BlockdaemonHQ), [Blockdaemon research paper](https://start.blockdaemon.com/hubfs/Downloads/Research%20Papers/Its%20Not%20About%20Slashing.pdf)).

## Incorporation and Operations

- **Incorporation jurisdiction:** **Delaware, United States — high confidence.** Bloomberg’s accredited LEI service records Blockdaemon Inc. as an active Delaware corporation under registration number 5417202 and states that the record is fully corroborated against the Delaware registration authority ([Bloomberg LEI](https://lei.bloomberg.com/leis/view/254900RSXBG1LL701F39)).
- **Principal operating base:** **United States, with a corporate headquarters/contact base in Los Angeles — medium-high confidence.** The LEI record gives a Los Angeles headquarters address, and Blockdaemon’s official privacy policy uses the same Los Angeles corporate contact location. Its public LinkedIn page instead characterizes the company as “Remote First, US,” so Los Angeles should not be read as the location of its entire workforce ([Bloomberg LEI](https://lei.bloomberg.com/leis/view/254900RSXBG1LL701F39), [Privacy Policy](https://www.blockdaemon.com/privacy-policy), [LinkedIn company page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/blockdaemon)).
- **Operating regions:** **United States, Europe, Asia-Pacific and the United Arab Emirates — high confidence.** Blockdaemon reports offices or entities in New York City, Newcastle, London, Galway, Singapore and Abu Dhabi, together with broader EMEA and APAC expansion ([Blockdaemon About](https://www.blockdaemon.com/about), [Abu Dhabi announcement](https://www.blockdaemon.com/blog/announcing-blockdaemons-strategic-expansion-into-abu-dhabi)).

## Activities

Blockdaemon supplies institutional blockchain infrastructure, including managed and dedicated nodes, validator and staking services, RPC and on-chain-data APIs, transaction services, and multiparty-computation wallets, vaults and key-management products. It identifies financial institutions, banks, custodians, exchanges, crypto platforms and developers as principal customer groups ([Blockdaemon About](https://www.blockdaemon.com/about), [Blockdaemon XRP services](https://www.blockdaemon.com/protocols/xrp)).

For XRP Ledger specifically, Blockdaemon offers managed dedicated nodes, APIs and white-label validators. That service model, the Blockdaemon-controlled subdomain, the validator metadata naming Blockdaemon’s founder, and the historical entity attribution collectively support Blockdaemon as the infrastructure provider associated with this validator ([Blockdaemon XRP services](https://www.blockdaemon.com/protocols/xrp), [Bithomp validator registry](https://bithomp.com/validators?amendment=fixXChainRewardRounding), [historical validator table](https://www.crypto-law.us/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/ex6_7_8.pdf)). Public evidence does not resolve whether Blockdaemon, Arrington XRP Capital or another customer ultimately controls the signing key.

## Public Footprint

**Tier: Medium.** Blockdaemon reports more than 400 institutional customers, over 70 global points of presence, more than 250,000 nodes launched and operations spanning multiple regions. Its LinkedIn company page reports a workforce range of 201–500, but this is a platform-supplied range rather than a verified current employee count ([Blockdaemon About](https://www.blockdaemon.com/about), [LinkedIn company page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/blockdaemon)). **Confidence: medium. Headcount is not established.**

## Business Summary

Blockdaemon Inc. is a privately held Delaware corporation that provides institutional blockchain infrastructure. Its principal U.S. operating base is identified in Los Angeles, while the company describes itself as remote-first and maintains a wider presence across the United States, Europe, Asia-Pacific and the United Arab Emirates. Its products and services include managed and dedicated blockchain nodes, validator and staking infrastructure, RPC and data APIs, transaction services, and multiparty-computation wallets, vaults and key-management tools. Customers and stakeholders include financial institutions, banks, custodians, exchanges, crypto platforms, developers and blockchain networks. The supplied XRP Ledger validator is publicly tied to a Blockdaemon-controlled subdomain and registry metadata identifies Blockdaemon personnel, although the “arrington-xrp-capital” label leaves the customer or sponsorship relationship unresolved. Its public institutional footprint is Medium; an exact headcount is not established.

## Uncertainty

- The claimed domain and its TOML file could not be accessed directly during this research; the key-to-domain verification is therefore reported from Bithomp rather than claimed as a direct inspection.
- The meaning of `arrington-xrp-capital` in the subdomain is unresolved. It may denote a customer, sponsor or historical deployment label, but no accessible primary source established the arrangement.
- Public evidence supports Blockdaemon as the associated infrastructure provider, but it does not establish which entity has ultimate custody or governance of the validator signing key.
- Blockdaemon’s official chronology says the company was established in 2017, while its LEI record gives a 2013 legal-entity creation date. The reason for this difference—such as an earlier entity later repurposed or renamed—is not established.
- The LEI record identifies Los Angeles as headquarters, whereas LinkedIn describes the organization as remote-first. Los Angeles is therefore treated as the corporate base, not necessarily the principal concentration of personnel.
- Exact current headcount, ownership structure and any former corporate names are not established.
