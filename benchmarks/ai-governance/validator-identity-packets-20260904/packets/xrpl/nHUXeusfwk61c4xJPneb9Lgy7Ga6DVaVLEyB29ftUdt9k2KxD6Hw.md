# Organization Profile

## Identity

The organization is **The Integrators B.V.**, a Dutch private limited-liability company (*besloten vennootschap*) registered under Chamber of Commerce number 64731464. **XRPL Labs** is its documented trade name, while **Xaman** is its current public-facing brand. The earlier name **Xumm** referred to the wallet product and is not treated here as a former legal entity name. [The company’s current privacy statement](https://xrpl-labs.com/static/conditions/privacy-statement-en.pdf) identifies The Integrators B.V. as the entity and XRPL Labs as its trade name; [its rebranding announcement](https://xaman.app/blog/xumm-evolves-into-a-new-era-welcome-xaman) documents the transition to Xaman.

The validator key `nHUXeusfwk61c4xJPneb9Lgy7Ga6DVaVLEyB29ftUdt9k2KxD6Hw` is associated by a public validator registry with `validator.xrpl-labs.com`, labeled “XRPL Labs Validator,” and marked as domain-verified through an XRP Ledger TOML file. [Bithomp’s validator registry](https://bithomp.com/validators) displays this exact key-domain pairing. Separately, [The Integrators’ official terms](https://xrpl-labs.com/static/documents/XRPL-Labs-Terms-of-Service-V1.pdf) state that the company runs XRPL protocol-chain infrastructure, including a validator role. Together these sources support organizational attribution, subject to the ownership caveat under Uncertainty.

## Activities

The Integrators develops **Xaman**, a self-custodial wallet supporting the XRP Ledger and Xahau. Its services include account creation and management, asset transfers, transaction tracking, token exchange, embedded third-party and company-developed xApps, Xaman Pro subscriptions, support modules, and Xaman Cards for hardware-based key management. Its users and stakeholders include individual wallet users, developers, third-party application providers, merchants, and participants in the XRPL and Xahau ecosystems. [Official terms](https://xrpl-labs.com/static/documents/XRPL-Labs-Terms-of-Service-V1.pdf), [Xaman developer site](https://xaman.app/developers)

The organization also develops open-source software, contributes tooling and testing work around XRPL software, supports ecosystem integrations, and operates network infrastructure. [XRPL Labs ecosystem overview](https://xaman.app/blog/xrpl-labs-powering-the-ecosystem-beyond-xaman), [official terms](https://xrpl-labs.com/static/documents/XRPL-Labs-Terms-of-Service-V1.pdf)

Its relationship to the specified validator is supported by the exact key-domain record in [Bithomp’s validator registry](https://bithomp.com/validators), the validator’s `xrpl-labs.com` subdomain, and the company’s own statement that it operates an XRPL validator role. The direct technical key-to-domain evidence is registry-mediated because the validator’s TOML endpoint was not accessible through the research interface.

## Business Summary

The Integrators B.V., trading as XRPL Labs and using the Xaman brand, is a Dutch private limited-liability software company incorporated and principally based in the Netherlands. It develops Xaman, a self-custodial mobile wallet for the XRP Ledger and Xahau, together with subscriptions, NFC signing cards, embedded xApps, developer tools, support services, and ledger network infrastructure. Its stakeholders include retail wallet users, software developers, third-party application providers, merchants, and the broader XRP Ledger and Xahau communities. The organization serves an international user base, although its current country-by-country operating coverage is not fully established. It is assessed as Small from an official public roster naming 11 team members; total legal headcount is not established. Public validator-registry and company legal materials connect it to the specified XRP Ledger validator.

## Uncertainty

- The validator’s live TOML endpoint and root page could not be accessed through the research interface. The exact key-domain connection therefore rests on Bithomp’s reported TOML verification rather than direct inspection of the TOML file.
- Bithomp displays Wietse Wind as the validator’s owner while labeling it “XRPL Labs Validator.” The company’s terms confirm that The Integrators operates a validator role but do not publish the validator key. The public evidence does not conclusively distinguish personal key custody from operational control by the company.
- “Xaman” is clearly the current public brand, but the latest legal documentation continues to identify the entity as The Integrators B.V. and the trade name as XRPL Labs. Xaman is therefore not presented as a separate legal entity.
- Current legal material identifies Amersfoort, whereas older company materials used Amsterdam-Duivendrecht. This appears consistent with an address change, but the effective date was not established.
- The official team page names 11 people but does not define employment status or certify that the roster is exhaustive. Formal headcount is not established.
- International user reach does not establish incorporation, offices, employees, licensing, or a permanent operating presence outside the Netherlands.
