# Organization Profile

## Identity

The validator’s public operator is identified as **Scrambled Egg Technologies, LLC**, a technology company using **XRPScan** as its public-facing product and operating name. XRPScan’s official footer displays “Scrambled Egg Technologies, LLC,” while its [Terms of Service](https://docs.xrpscan.com/help/terms-of-service) and [Privacy Policy](https://docs.xrpscan.com/help/privacy-policy) identify “Scrambled Egg Technologies” as the operator of xrpscan.com and call the service “XRPScan.” The exact registered legal name and LLC formation remain unverified because no authoritative incorporation filing was located.

The connection to the validator is direct and high-confidence: XRPScan’s [official validator documentation](https://docs.xrpscan.com/help/validator) states that it runs an XRP Ledger validator at xrpscan.com and gives the exact master key `nHDB2PAPYqF86j9j3c6w1F1ZqwvQfiWcFShZ9Pokg9q4ohNDSkAz`. Its [verified GitHub organization](https://github.com/xrpscan) independently confirms that the XRPScan organization controls xrpscan.com.

Supported alias: **XRPScan / XRPSCAN**. No former names were established.

## Activities

Scrambled Egg Technologies operates XRPScan, a blockchain explorer and analytics platform for the XRP Ledger. Its services expose transaction details, accounts, balances, ledgers, tokens, NFTs, amendments, metrics, charts, and related network data ([Terms of Service](https://docs.xrpscan.com/help/terms-of-service); [official explorer](https://devnet.xrpscan.com/)). It offers public and commercial REST API access, including free, usage-priced, and enterprise plans aimed at developers, students, researchers, businesses, and high-volume users ([API introduction](https://docs.xrpscan.com/api-documentation/introduction); [pricing](https://docs.xrpscan.com/api-documentation/pricing)).

The organization also maintains advanced ledger-search tooling and public software projects, including an XRPL deep-search platform, an XRPL Go client, and Hermes, a service for storing and relaying validation messages ([advanced-search API](https://docs.xrpscan.com/advanced-search/advanced-search-api); [GitHub organization](https://github.com/xrpscan); [Hermes repository](https://github.com/xrpscan/hermes)). It also operates Xahscan, an explorer for the Xahau ledger ([Xahscan](https://xahscan.com/)).

Its relationship to the subject validator is operational: XRPScan expressly says it runs the validator and publishes the supplied key in its [validator documentation](https://docs.xrpscan.com/help/validator).

## Business Summary

Scrambled Egg Technologies, LLC is the public company name displayed by XRPScan, although its registered legal status and incorporation jurisdiction are not established in accessible primary records. Operating under the XRPScan name, it provides an XRP Ledger block explorer, network analytics, advanced ledger search, REST APIs, and related open-source data infrastructure. Its users and stakeholders include individual ledger users, developers, researchers, exchanges, wallets, financial institutions, and enterprises that need transaction, account, token, validator, and network data. The service is internet-based and global in reach. India appears to be its principal operating base, with Mumbai reported by a business-information source and Indian law governing its terms, but the company does not publish a verified office location. Its public footprint is Micro: public evidence points to a founder-led organization with a compact institutional presence, while employee headcount remains unestablished. The organization publicly states that it independently operates the identified XRP Ledger validator.
