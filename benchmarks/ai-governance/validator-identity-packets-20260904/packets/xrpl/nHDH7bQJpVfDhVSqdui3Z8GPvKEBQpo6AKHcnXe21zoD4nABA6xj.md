# Organization Profile

## Identity

The organization is identified with **moderate confidence** as the **University of Waterloo**, a Canadian public research university, registered charity and statutory corporation. Ontario lists it among the province’s public universities, while the university states that its legal name is “University of Waterloo” and that it belongs to Ontario’s broader public sector. [Ontario public-university directory](https://www.ontario.ca/page/ontario-universities), [University legal-name page](https://uwaterloo.ca/support/give-to-waterloo/how-to-give/wills-and-future-gifts), [University Finance description](https://uwaterloo.ca/finance/about-finance)

Supported public aliases are **Waterloo** and **UWaterloo**, both used by the institution itself. No former legal name was established. [University overview](https://uwaterloo.ca/about/), [University budget document](https://uwaterloo.ca/waterloo-budget-plan/sites/default/files/uploads/documents/2025-04-15-board-of-governors-meeting-book-open.pdf)

## Incorporation and Operations

- **Incorporation jurisdiction:** **Ontario, Canada — high confidence.** The University of Waterloo Act, 1972 continues the University of Waterloo as a body corporate under that name; university financial materials state that it was originally incorporated in 1959 under provincial legislation. [University of Waterloo Act](https://uwaterloo.ca/secretariat/university-waterloo-act), [audited financial statements](https://uwaterloo.ca/finance/sites/default/files/uploads/documents/april-30-2022.pdf?r470=)
- **Principal operating region:** **Waterloo, Ontario, Canada — high confidence.** Its main campus and institutional address are in Waterloo. It also operates satellite campuses in Cambridge, Kitchener and Stratford, all in Ontario. [University campus directory](https://uwaterloo.ca/about/maps-and-directions), [official contact page](https://uwaterloo.ca/about/contact-us)

The university has international academic and stakeholder reach through students, alumni, research relationships and co-operative employers, but the supported physical campus footprint identified here is concentrated in southwestern Ontario. [University quick facts](https://uwaterloo.ca/about/facts)

## Activities

The University of Waterloo provides undergraduate and graduate education, research, co-operative and work-integrated learning, and entrepreneurship and commercialization support. Its primary stakeholders include students, faculty, staff, research partners, governments, nonprofit organizations, donors, alumni and employers. The institution reports more than 1,160 research partnerships and over 8,000 co-op employers across more than 70 countries. [University overview](https://uwaterloo.ca/about/), [University quick facts](https://uwaterloo.ca/about/facts)

Its documented blockchain work includes research on consensus protocols, cryptography, privacy, distributed systems, scalability and smart contracts. It participated in Ripple’s University Blockchain Research Initiative and hosted a Ripple-supported research chair and research funding. [Blockchain Research program](https://uwaterloo.ca/blockchain-research/), [University announcement](https://uwaterloo.ca/engineering/news/waterloo-joins-worlds-research-leaders-advance-blockchain)

The supplied validator key is publicly associated with `ripplevalidator.uwaterloo.ca`, and university researchers published a technical report based on measurements of an XRP validator. However, the available sources do not establish which faculty, laboratory or administrative unit operates the identified validator. [Bithomp validator registry](https://bithomp.com/validators?amendment=Credentials), [Energy Consumption Analysis of XRP Validator](https://uwspace.uwaterloo.ca/items/e81bb640-2511-45c9-82a2-f1a8282ab346)

## Public Footprint

**Very large.** High confidence; headcount is established. An official 2026 university budget document reports **4,611 employees** as of January 2026, comprising 1,684 faculty and 2,927 staff. The university also reports more than 41,000 students and over 263,000 alumni internationally. [2026–27 operating-budget materials](https://uwaterloo.ca/secretariat/sites/default/files/uploads/documents/_2026-03-26-senate-planning-finance-agenda-package.pdf), [University quick facts](https://uwaterloo.ca/about/facts)

## Business Summary

University of Waterloo is a Canadian public research university and registered charity constituted as an Ontario statutory corporation. It is incorporated in Ontario and is principally based in Waterloo, with additional campuses in Cambridge, Kitchener and Stratford, all in Ontario. The institution provides undergraduate and graduate education, large-scale co-operative and work-integrated learning, and research and commercialization programs. Its stakeholders include students, faculty and staff, research partners, governments, nonprofit organizations, donors, alumni and employers. Its reach is international through students, alumni, partnerships and co-op employers, while its physical campus base is in southwestern Ontario. The institution falls in the Very large public-footprint tier, supported by more than 41,000 students, 4,611 employees and 263,000-plus alumni. Public validator data associates the supplied key with a University of Waterloo subdomain, although the responsible internal unit is not established.
