# Organization Profile

## Identity

**Canonical name:** Institute for Information Sciences (I2S), University of Kansas.

**Entity type:** An unincorporated university research institute within the University of Kansas, a public institution governed by the Kansas Board of Regents. Kansas law classifies university academic and administrative units as unincorporated subdivisions and identifies the University of Kansas as a state educational institution. ([Kansas Statutes §76-718b](https://www.kslegislature.gov/b2025_26/laws/076_000_0000_chapter/076_007_0000_article/076_007_0018b_section/076_007_0018b_k/), [Kansas Statutes §76-711](https://www.kslegislature.gov/b2023_24/laws/076_000_0000_chapter/076_007_0000_article/076_007_0011_section/076_007_0011_k/), [official KU/I2S profile](https://i2s-research.ku.edu/people/michael-hulet))

**Supported names:**

- **I2S** — official abbreviation for the Institute for Information Sciences.
- **Information and Telecommunication Technology Center (ITTC)** — former name; KU states that ITTC changed its name to I2S in 2022. ([official rename announcement](https://i2s-research.ku.edu/news/article/2022/12/23/ittcbecomesi2s))

## Incorporation and Operations

**Incorporation jurisdiction:** Not applicable as a standalone corporate incorporation — **high confidence**. I2S is a university unit, and Kansas law describes such units as unincorporated subdivisions of their university. Its parent, the University of Kansas, is a Kansas state educational institution rather than a privately incorporated company. ([Kansas Statutes §76-718b](https://www.kslegislature.gov/b2025_26/laws/076_000_0000_chapter/076_007_0000_article/076_007_0018b_section/076_007_0018b_k/), [Kansas Statutes §76-711](https://www.kslegislature.gov/b2023_24/laws/076_000_0000_chapter/076_007_0000_article/076_007_0011_section/076_007_0011_k/))

**Principal operating base:** Nichols Hall, University of Kansas, Lawrence, Kansas, United States — **high confidence**. The institute’s official site places I2S in Nichols Hall, while its staff profiles give Lawrence as the location. ([I2S website](https://i2s-research.ku.edu/), [official staff profile](https://i2s-research.ku.edu/people/michael-hulet))

**Operating reach:** Kansas-focused with national and international research and service relationships — **medium confidence**. I2S describes its mission in terms of regional and national service and international leadership in information infrastructure; this describes institutional reach rather than separately established foreign offices. ([I2S impact report page](https://i2s-research.ku.edu/2024-impact-report))

## Activities

I2S creates, disseminates, and supports commercialization of technologies in computer systems, communication systems, and radar systems. ([official I2S website](https://i2s-research.ku.edu/)) Its six research centers cover communications and photonics, cyber-social dynamics, computer systems, high-assurance and secure systems, mathematical and interdisciplinary computing, and radar systems. ([official centers directory](https://i2s-research.ku.edu/i2s-research-centers))

Its stakeholders include students and researchers, government and industry sponsors, technology partners, and communities served through education, workforce development, research, and technology transfer. Its published project portfolio identifies sponsors including the National Science Foundation, universities, government contractors, and technology companies. ([official projects directory](https://i2s-research.ku.edu/i2s-projects))

The validator relationship is supported by the exact key-to-domain record, the domain’s use of the institute’s former `ittc` name, the named operator’s official I2S employment record, and KU’s documented Ripple-supported blockchain research. ([validator record](https://bithomp.com/validator/nHUVPzAmAmQ2QSc4oE1iLfsGi17qN2ado8PhxvgEkou76FLxAz7C), [operator profile](https://i2s-research.ku.edu/people/michael-hulet), [KU Ripple announcement](https://eecs.ku.edu/news/article/2019/03/20/ripple-supports-blockchain-research-ku-2m-gift))

## Business Summary

The Institute for Information Sciences (I2S) is an unincorporated research institute of the University of Kansas, a Kansas public university. Formerly called the Information and Telecommunication Technology Center (ITTC), it principally operates from Nichols Hall in Lawrence, Kansas. I2S conducts basic and applied research, education, and technology transfer in computer systems, communications and networking, radar and remote sensing, cybersecurity, mathematical computing, and technology’s social effects. Its principal stakeholders include university students and faculty, government and industry research sponsors, technology partners, and regional and national communities served by its research and workforce-development mission. Its work has state, national, and international reach. The institute’s public footprint is Medium: its current directory spans more than 50 faculty, staff, students, researchers, and affiliates, though a standalone employee headcount is not established. Public evidence links its former ITTC domain to the XRP Ledger validator.

## Uncertainty

- The validator host’s TOML file was not directly accessible during this research. The key-to-domain connection therefore relies on Bithomp’s verified-TOML record rather than an independently retrieved copy of the file.
- Public sources name Michael Hulet as operator and identify him as I2S’s senior network systems administrator, but they do not establish who has ultimate legal custody or administrative control of the validator key: Hulet personally, I2S, central KU administration, or another KU unit.
- The validator hostname retains `ittc`, the institute’s pre-2022 name. No evidence indicates that ITTC remains a separate entity after the I2S renaming.
- Some official materials use “Telecommunication” and others “Telecommunications” in expanding ITTC. This appears to be naming variation, not evidence of separate organizations.
- I2S has no separately established corporate incorporation or employee headcount. Directory participation includes employees, students, researchers, and affiliates.
