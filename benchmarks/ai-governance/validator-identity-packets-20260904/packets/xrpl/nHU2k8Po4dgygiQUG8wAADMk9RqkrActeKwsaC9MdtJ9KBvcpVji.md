# Organization Profile

## Identity

The validator operator is **Eminence Limited**, styled **Eminence Ltd.**, an Isle of Man limited company. Its supported public names are **Eminence Holdings**, used on the organization’s current website, and **CasinoCoin**, recorded as a trading/business name and presented as its flagship product. No former legal name is established. The historical CasinoCoin Foundation was described as having been replaced by Eminence, so it is not treated as an alias or former name. The operator-controlled validator page directly pairs Eminence Ltd. with public key `nHU2k8Po4dgygiQUG8wAADMk9RqkrActeKwsaC9MdtJ9KBvcpVji`, while the XRP Ledger Foundation independently described `verum.eminence.im` as belonging to the CasinoCoin issuer. ([validator page](https://verum.eminence.im/), [XRPL Foundation](https://blog.xrplf.org/xrplf-unl-update-jul-21), [official Eminence website](https://eminence.im/), [register extract](https://endontech.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/regcp_16august2021.pdf))

## Official Web Presence

Official website: [https://eminence.im/](https://eminence.im/). The validator subdomain links to Eminence legal pages, and Eminence’s site identifies CasinoCoin as its flagship project. ([validator page](https://verum.eminence.im/), [Eminence](https://eminence.im/))

Official X handle: [@CasinoCoin](https://x.com/CasinoCoin), the organization’s CasinoCoin project account. The domain-verified official CasinoCoin GitHub organization identifies this handle; no separate Eminence corporate X account was established. ([official GitHub organization](https://github.com/casinocoin))

## Incorporation and Operations

**Incorporation jurisdiction — Isle of Man (high confidence).** An Isle of Man data-controller register extract identifies Eminence Limited, company number `134232C`, and explicitly states its country of registration as the Isle of Man. Current official CasinoCoin legal pages continue to associate Eminence Ltd. and that company number with Isle of Man law and jurisdiction. ([register extract](https://endontech.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/regcp_16august2021.pdf), [terms of service](https://casinocoin.im/terms-of-service/))

**Principal operating base — Douglas, Isle of Man (high confidence).** Eminence’s validator page, official legal pages, and public company profile consistently place the organization in Douglas. Its services are marketed internationally to consumers, businesses, public entities, and gaming operators, but no separately established operating offices outside the Isle of Man were found. ([validator page](https://verum.eminence.im/), [privacy policy](https://casinocoin.im/privacy-policy/), [Eminence](https://eminence.im/))

## Activities

Eminence describes itself as a blockchain technology provider developing transaction and payment solutions using the XRP Ledger and other blockchain infrastructure. Its stated audiences include individuals, financial institutions, businesses, public entities, online-gaming operators, and players. CasinoCoin is its principal publicly documented product: a gaming-oriented digital currency available on XRPL and Solana, accompanied by the CasinoCoin Lobby wallet application and the PontiX multichain bridge. ([Eminence](https://eminence.im/), [CasinoCoin product site](https://www.csc-xrpl.com/))

The relationship to the validator is direct: the claimed domain displays the supplied public key beside Eminence Ltd.’s name, and the XRP Ledger Foundation identified the validator as belonging to the CasinoCoin issuer. ([validator page](https://verum.eminence.im/), [XRPL Foundation](https://blog.xrplf.org/xrplf-unl-update-jul-21))

**Small (11–50).** Confidence: **medium**. Exact current headcount is not independently established. ([Eminence Holdings profile](https://im.linkedin.com/company/eminence-holdings), [official team article](https://casinocoin.im/casinocoin-meet-the-team/), [partners](https://casinocoin.im/partners/))

## Business Summary

Eminence Limited, publicly branded as Eminence Holdings and trading through CasinoCoin, is an Isle of Man limited company and blockchain technology provider incorporated and principally based in Douglas. It develops blockchain-based transaction and payment infrastructure, including the CasinoCoin digital currency for regulated gaming, a wallet-integrated casino lobby, and multichain transfer tools. Its stated customer and stakeholder groups include individual users, online gaming operators, financial institutions, other businesses, and public entities. Its marketed reach is international, although no separately established operating offices outside the Isle of Man were found. Its public institutional footprint is classified as Small; exact headcount is not independently established.

## Uncertainty

The current Eminence homepage uses **Eminence Holdings** and copyrights **Eminence Holdings Limited**, while the validator page and current CasinoCoin legal pages use **Eminence Ltd.**, and the register extract records **Eminence Limited**. No current registry detail establishing a formal legal-name change was retrieved, so Eminence Holdings is treated as a supported public name rather than a proven replacement legal name.

A separate corporate X account for Eminence was not established; `@CasinoCoin` is supported only as the official product/project account. Exact current headcount, corporate status in the live Isle of Man register, and operating offices outside Douglas remain unestablished. CasinoCoin’s current site also announces migration to LHT, leaving the longer-term scope of the CasinoCoin-branded products unresolved. ([CasinoCoin](https://casinocoin.im/))
