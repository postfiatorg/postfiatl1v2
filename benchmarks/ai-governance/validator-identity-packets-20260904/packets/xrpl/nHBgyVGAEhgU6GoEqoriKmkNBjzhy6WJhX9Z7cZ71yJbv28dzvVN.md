## Activities

XRPL Commons supports the XRP Ledger ecosystem through blockchain education, developer training, hackathons, university partnerships, research, community events, and the Aquarium residency and incubation program. Its stated stakeholders include developers, educators, entrepreneurs, researchers, universities, enterprises, and the broader XRPL community. [Official homepage](https://www.xrpl-commons.org/)

It also publishes or develops open-source software, developer tooling, infrastructure projects, educational platforms, and Glow, a platform for nominating, evaluating, verifying, and disbursing grants to XRPL contributors. [XRPL Commons product catalog](https://products.xrpl-commons.org/), [Glow terms](https://glow-docs.xrpl-commons.org/documents/terms-of-service), [official GitHub organization](https://github.com/XRPL-Commons)

Its relationship to the supplied validator is supported by the exact public-key mapping to a subdomain of its official domain. [XRPLF xrpl-cli documentation](https://github.com/XRPLF/xrpl-cli) Day-to-day technical control and whether operation is performed directly by staff or a service provider are not publicly established.

## Business Summary

XRPL Commons is a French nonprofit association incorporated in France and principally based in Paris. It supports the XRP Ledger ecosystem through blockchain education, developer training, hackathons, community and university programs, research, open-source software and infrastructure, grants, and the Aquarium builder residency and incubation program. Its primary stakeholders are developers, entrepreneurs, educators, researchers, universities, enterprises, and other participants in the XRPL ecosystem. Its programs and community engagement are presented as international, while its institutional and operational center is Paris. Its public footprint falls in the Small tier, based on a public roster of more than ten team roles and an organization-controlled LinkedIn classification of 11–50 employees; however, exact current headcount is not established.
