# Organization Profile

## Identity

**Canonical name:** Australian National University. The regulator-recorded trading/public name is **The Australian National University**, abbreviated **ANU**. It is an Australian statutory public university, a body corporate under the *Australian National University Act 1991*, and a registered charity. [Federal legislation](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A04206/latest/text), [ABN Lookup](https://abr.business.gov.au/ABN/View/52%20234%20063%20906), and [TEQSA’s provider register](https://www.teqsa.gov.au/provider/australian-national-university) support these classifications.

**Supported aliases:** ANU; The Australian National University. **Former names:** Not established.

**Validator connection:** Bithomp’s public validator registry maps the exact key `nHUbgDd63HiuP68VRWazKwZRzS61N37K3NbfQaZLhSQ24LGGmjtn` to `xrp-col.anu.edu.au`. [Bithomp validator registry](https://bithomp.com/validators?amendment=fixAMMv1_3). ANU states that it licenses `anu.edu.au`, registers subordinate names through its DNS infrastructure, and requires services conducted as part of University business to use that domain. [ANU domain-naming requirements](https://services.anu.edu.au/information-technology/infrastructure/domain-name-service/naming-requirements). This supports attribution to ANU at the institutional level, but the responsible school, department, administrator, or individual operator is not established.

## Incorporation and Operations

**Incorporation jurisdiction — High confidence:** Commonwealth of Australia. ANU is continued as a body corporate under the federal *Australian National University Act 1991*; the Act also identifies the earlier founding statute as the *Australian National University Act 1946*. [Federal Register of Legislation](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A04206/2014-07-01/2014-07-01/text/1/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html).

**Principal operating base — High confidence:** Canberra, Australian Capital Territory. ANU’s official contact page gives Canberra as its address, while TEQSA records its head office in Acton, ACT. [ANU contact page](https://www.anu.edu.au/contact-anu), [TEQSA provider register](https://www.teqsa.gov.au/provider/australian-national-university).

**Other operating regions — High confidence:** Additional teaching and research facilities are located elsewhere in the ACT, New South Wales, and the Northern Territory, including Kioloa, Mount Stromlo, the North Australian Research Unit, Siding Spring Observatory, and Spring Valley Farm. [ANU campuses and facilities](https://www.anu.edu.au/about/campuses-facilities). Its institutional remit and engagement extend nationally and internationally, particularly toward the Asia-Pacific region. [ANU facts](https://www.anu.edu.au/about/facts-about-anu).

## Activities

ANU provides undergraduate and postgraduate education, undertakes research, supplies research facilities, awards academic qualifications, and performs public-engagement and extension activities. These functions are defined in its governing federal statute. [Australian National University Act 1991](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A04206/2014-07-01/2014-07-01/text/1/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html).

Its principal stakeholders include Australian and international students, researchers, governments, private-sector and research partners, alumni, and the wider community. ANU describes research, education, and policy engagement as its core roles and reports collaboration with government, industry, and research institutions. [About ANU](https://www.anu.edu.au/about), [ANU strategic initiatives](https://www.anu.edu.au/about/strategic-initiatives).

Regarding the validator, the supported activity is institutional participation in XRP Ledger infrastructure through a validator associated by registry data with an ANU subdomain. The record does not establish which internal unit runs it or whether validator operation is part of a broader ANU blockchain program. ANU separately documents research and education concerning blockchain, cryptocurrency, smart contracts, and digital payments, but those programs are not direct proof of operational responsibility for this key. [ANU Ripple University Blockchain Research Initiative](https://law.anu.edu.au/ripple-university-blockchain-research-initiative).

## Public Footprint

**Very large — High confidence.** ANU reports 10,252 undergraduate and 7,128 postgraduate students drawn from Australia and 100 countries, operates multiple campuses and research facilities, and maintains six academic colleges containing numerous schools and research centres. [ANU facts](https://www.anu.edu.au/about/facts-about-anu), [About ANU](https://www.anu.edu.au/about), [ANU campuses and facilities](https://www.anu.edu.au/about/campuses-facilities). A current employee headcount was not established; the tier is based on the documented student population, multi-campus structure, national statutory remit, and international institutional reach.

## Business Summary

Australian National University, publicly styled The Australian National University and abbreviated ANU, is an Australian statutory public university and body corporate registered as a charity. It is constituted under Commonwealth legislation, with its principal base in Canberra, Australian Capital Territory, and additional teaching or research facilities in the ACT, New South Wales and the Northern Territory. ANU provides undergraduate and postgraduate education, conducts multidisciplinary research, awards qualifications and contributes expertise to public policy. Its stakeholders include domestic and international students, researchers, governments, private-sector and research partners, alumni and the wider community, with national, Asia-Pacific and international reach. Its public footprint is Very large, supported by more than 17,000 enrolled students and a broad multi-campus institutional structure; a current employee headcount was not established. Public validator registry data links the specified XRPL key to an ANU-controlled subdomain, although the responsible internal unit and operator remain unconfirmed.
