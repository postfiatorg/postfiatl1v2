# Organization Profile

## Identity

The validator is attributable with high confidence to **Anodos Labs Inc.**, a financial-technology corporation. The company’s legal terms identify Anodos Labs Inc. as the operator of `anodos.finance`, while an SEC filing identifies the same entity as a Wyoming corporation and names that website ([Anodos Terms of Service](https://anodos.finance/terms-of-services), [SEC Form C-U](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2064519/000206451926000002/xslC_X01/primary_doc.xml)).

Supported names are **Anodos** and **Anodos Labs**. A Wyoming certificate included in the company’s SEC offering materials records that **Anodos LLC** converted into Anodos Labs Inc. in January 2025, supporting it as a former legal name/form ([SEC Form C exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2064519/000206451926000001/anodos.pdf)).

The validator connection is direct: the company’s official infrastructure subdomain labels the service “Anodos Infrastructure” and publishes the exact supplied XRPL validator key, `nHUr8EhgKeTc9ESNt4nMYzWC2Pu7GgRHMRTsNEyGBTCfnHPxmXcm` ([Anodos Infrastructure](https://infra.anodos.finance/)).

## Official Web Presence

- **Official website:** [https://anodos.finance/](https://anodos.finance/) — identified as the issuer’s website in SEC filings and as a service operated by Anodos Labs Inc. in the company’s legal terms ([SEC Form C-U](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2064519/000206451926000002/xslC_X01/primary_doc.xml), [Terms of Service](https://anodos.finance/terms-of-services)).
- **Official X handle:** [@AnodosFinance](https://x.com/AnodosFinance) — repeatedly linked as the company account by Anodos’s own website ([official company newsletter](https://anodos.finance/news/newsletter-june-2026)). The X profile itself was not directly accessible during this research.

## Incorporation and Operations

- **Incorporation jurisdiction:** Wyoming, United States — **high confidence**. SEC filings identify Anodos Labs Inc. as a Wyoming corporation; the associated state certificate records conversion from Anodos LLC to a Wyoming profit corporation in January 2025 ([SEC Form C-U](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2064519/000206451926000002/xslC_X01/primary_doc.xml), [SEC Form C exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2064519/000206451926000001/anodos.pdf)).
- **Declared principal place of business:** Sheridan, Wyoming, United States — **high confidence as a filing declaration**. The company reported Sheridan as its principal place of business on Form D ([SEC Form D](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2064519/000206451925000001/xslFormDX01/primary_doc.xml)).
- **Principal operating footprint:** Thessaloniki, Greece, together with a distributed remote team — **medium confidence**. The company-managed LinkedIn page lists Thessaloniki as a location, while Anodos’s official About page describes a remote-only work model and documents team activity in Thessaloniki ([LinkedIn company page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/anodosfinance), [Anodos About](https://anodos.finance/about)). Whether Sheridan or Thessaloniki functions as the primary day-to-day management center is not conclusively established.

## Activities

Anodos develops non-custodial blockchain and financial software. Its published solutions include an XRP Ledger decentralized-exchange interface, a passkey-based wallet, a Xaman swap application, and a planned consumer and SME neobank/payment application ([Anodos Solutions](https://anodos.finance/solutions), [Anodos product portal](https://apps.anodos.finance/)). Its stated users include consumers, freelancers, investors, digital-asset participants, and small and medium-sized businesses seeking payments, asset management, trading, liquidity, and wallet tools ([official website](https://anodos.finance/), [ANODEX documentation](https://docs.anodos.finance/products/anodex)).

The company also operates blockchain infrastructure. Its official infrastructure page expressly associates Anodos Labs with the supplied XRPL validator key, establishing the organizational relationship without relying on a third-party validator mapping ([Anodos Infrastructure](https://infra.anodos.finance/)).

## Public Footprint

**Small (11–50).** **Confidence: Medium. Headcount is not established.** The official About page presents four named team leaders plus ten additional team members, while the company-managed LinkedIn page exposes 19 associated profiles ([Anodos About](https://anodos.finance/about), [LinkedIn company page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/anodosfinance)). However, that LinkedIn page also self-classifies the company as having 2–10 employees, and a May 2026 SEC Form C reported zero current employees ([SEC Form C](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2064519/000206451926000001/xslC_X01/primary_doc.xml)). These figures may count employees, contractors, executives, or contributors differently. The tier therefore reflects the visible institutional team and product footprint rather than a verified payroll count.

## Business Summary

Anodos Labs Inc. is a privately held financial technology corporation incorporated in Wyoming, United States. It publicly declares Sheridan, Wyoming, as its principal place of business, while its observable operating footprint also includes Thessaloniki, Greece, and a distributed remote team. The company develops non-custodial blockchain software, including an XRP Ledger-based decentralized exchange, wallet and swap tools, and a planned neobank and payments application. Its intended users include consumers, freelancers, investors, digital-asset participants, and small and medium-sized businesses, with a stated global market orientation. Anodos also supports XRPL infrastructure through the validator tied to the supplied public key. Its public institutional footprint is Small, although exact employee headcount is not established because public disclosures use inconsistent workforce measures.

## Uncertainty

- The corporate chronology is not perfectly aligned: the 2025 Form D gives 2023 as the organization year, whereas later Form C materials record a January 2025 corporate date and conversion from Anodos LLC. The most plausible explanation is continuity through conversion, but the original LLC’s formation record was not independently examined.
- Sheridan is the legally declared principal place of business, but the evidence does not establish how much operational activity occurs there. Thessaloniki has a documented team and leadership footprint, while the company describes itself as remote-only.
- Exact employee headcount is unresolved. The SEC filing’s zero-employee figure conflicts with the company website’s displayed team and LinkedIn’s workforce indicators, potentially because the sources count contractors, executives, and employees differently.
- The official X handle is supported by the company website, but the X profile could not be directly accessed.
- No unsupported relationship was assigned to similarly named organizations in Poland, the United Kingdom, or elsewhere.
