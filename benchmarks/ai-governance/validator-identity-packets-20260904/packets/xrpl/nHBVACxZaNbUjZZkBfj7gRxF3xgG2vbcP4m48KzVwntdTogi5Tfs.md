# Organization Profile

## Identity

**Canonical public name:** Bidds. Its official documentation describes Bidds as “originally known as onXRP” and says the onXRP team launched the renamed, expanded platform. **onXRP** is therefore a supported former public name. Bidds is described as powered by XPUNKS, but XPUNKS is not established as an alias or the registered operator. The exact registered legal name and legal form are **Not established**; the supported entity type is a privately operated blockchain technology platform or business. ([Bidds documentation](https://docs.bidds.com/), [Bidds website](https://bidds.com/))

The validator connection is supported by an exact-key registry record associating `nHBVACxZaNbUjZZkBfj7gRxF3xgG2vbcP4m48KzVwntdTogi5Tfs` with the verified domain `onxrp.com`. XRP Ledger documentation explains that validator-domain verification establishes a two-way cryptographic association between a domain and validator key. Bidds’ current public X metadata also identifies the platform as an XRP Ledger validator. Together with Bidds’ documented succession from onXRP, this establishes the public-brand identity with high confidence, although it does not identify the underlying legal entity. ([Bithomp validator record](https://bithomp.com/validators?amendment=fixXChainRewardRounding), [XRPL domain-verification specification](https://xrpl.org/docs/references/xrp-ledger-toml), [Bidds X account](https://x.com/biddsonxrpl))

## Official Web Presence

**Official website:** [https://bidds.com/](https://bidds.com/) — high confidence. It is the active marketplace and is identified as Bidds’ website by the organization’s documentation and institutional profile. `onxrp.com` is the legacy and validator-verification domain associated with the former onXRP name. ([Bidds documentation](https://docs.bidds.com/), [Bidds LinkedIn page](https://ch.linkedin.com/company/bidds))

**Official X handle:** [@biddsonxrpl](https://x.com/biddsonxrpl) — medium confidence. Publicly indexed profile metadata identifies the account as `bidds.com`, links it to the website, and describes the platform as an XRP Ledger validator. The X page itself was not retrievable during this research session. The historical handle `@onXRPdotcom` appears in an older onXRP project listing but is not treated as the current handle. ([Current indexed profile](https://x.com/biddsonxrpl), [historical BitMart listing](https://www.bitmart.com/en-US/support/articles/7923014477723/360001026214/6198010642459))

## Incorporation and Operations

**Incorporation jurisdiction:** **Not established**; confidence in any specific jurisdiction is low. No accessible filing, registry entry, or official legal page identified a registered entity or incorporation jurisdiction.

**Principal operating base:** Zug, Switzerland — low-to-moderate confidence. Bidds’ self-reported LinkedIn organization page lists Zug as its headquarters, but this was not corroborated by a corporate registry or legal page. ([Bidds LinkedIn page](https://ch.linkedin.com/company/bidds))

The validator record reports Portugal as `owner_country`. The XRP Ledger specification defines that field as the main legal jurisdiction to which the validator owner is subject, but it does not prove company incorporation or the organization’s principal operating base. The difference between the Portuguese validator metadata and the reported Swiss headquarters remains unresolved. ([Bithomp validator record](https://bithomp.com/validators?amendment=fixXChainRewardRounding), [XRPL TOML field definitions](https://xrpl.org/docs/references/xrp-ledger-toml))

Operationally, Bidds serves online communities using the XRP Ledger and Coreum; these are technological markets rather than evidence of geographic offices. ([Bidds multichain documentation](https://docs.bidds.com/bidds/bidds-xrp-ledger-and-coreum))

## Activities

Bidds operates a multichain NFT marketplace for XRP Ledger and Coreum assets. Its documented services include collection discovery, primary-sale launchpads, secondary-market trading, bulk transactions, analytics, community features, cross-chain functionality, and the OXP utility token. Its website also presents an XRP Ledger swap or decentralized-exchange service. ([Marketplace documentation](https://docs.bidds.com/bidds/the-nft-marketplace), [OXP documentation](https://docs.bidds.com/bidds/the-oxp-token), [Bidds website](https://bidds.com/), [Bidds DEX](https://dex.bidds.com/))

The stated audience includes NFT creators, projects, companies, artists, builders, developers, collectors, experienced traders, and first-time blockchain users. Its relationship to the specified validator is institutional: the legacy onXRP domain is cryptographically associated with the key, while the successor Bidds brand publicly identifies itself as an XRP Ledger validator. ([Bidds introduction](https://docs.bidds.com/), [Bithomp validator record](https://bithomp.com/validators?amendment=fixXChainRewardRounding))

This supports the tier with moderate confidence, but an exact or independently verified headcount is **not established**. ([Bidds LinkedIn page](https://ch.linkedin.com/company/bidds))

## Business Summary

Bidds, formerly known as onXRP, is a privately operated blockchain technology platform whose exact legal entity and legal form are not publicly established in the reviewed sources. Its incorporation jurisdiction is not established. The organization reports Zug, Switzerland, as its headquarters, although this has not been independently confirmed and validator metadata separately identifies Portugal as the owner’s legal jurisdiction. Bidds provides a multichain NFT marketplace, launchpad, secondary-market trading, analytics and community features, alongside an XRP Ledger decentralized exchange or swap service and the OXP utility token. Its users and stakeholders include NFT creators, projects, companies, developers, collectors, traders and newcomers to blockchain applications. The platform primarily addresses the XRP Ledger and Coreum ecosystems. Its public footprint is assessed as Small, based on a self-reported 11–50 employee band; independently verified headcount is not established.

## Uncertainty

- The registered legal entity, legal form, ownership structure, and incorporation jurisdiction are not established.
- Zug is a self-reported headquarters, not a registry-confirmed principal operating base.
- Portugal appears only as the validator’s `owner_country`; it was excluded as proof of incorporation or physical operations.
- The precise role of Kaj Leroy in legal ownership or validator control is not established. Public metadata associates him with onXRP/Bidds but does not substitute for a filing.
- XPUNKS is described as powering Bidds, but whether it is a separate project, operating group, or legal owner is unresolved; it is not treated as an alias.
- The current X handle is supported by indexed profile metadata, but direct access to the primary X page was unavailable.
- The exact headcount is not established; the size assessment relies on a self-reported employee band.
