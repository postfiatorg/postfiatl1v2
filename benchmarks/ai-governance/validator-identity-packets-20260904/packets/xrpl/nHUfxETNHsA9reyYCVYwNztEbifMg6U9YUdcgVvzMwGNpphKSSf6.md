# Organization Profile

## Identity

**Canonical public name:** XRP Kuwait. The official site also uses **XRPK** and **XRPKuwait** as public stylings; no former name is established. The site describes the operation as independent infrastructure “operated by XRPK,” but does not identify a registered company, foundation, nonprofit, or other legal person. The best-supported entity classification is therefore an **individual-operated XRP Ledger infrastructure project**, not an established legal organization ([XRP Kuwait](https://xrpkuwait.com/)).

The validator connection is strong: Bithomp associates public key `nHUfxETNHsA9reyYCVYwNztEbifMg6U9YUdcgVvzMwGNpphKSSf6` with “XRP Kuwait” and reports `xrpkuwait.com` as cryptographically verified through its XRP Ledger TOML file ([Bithomp Validators](https://bithomp.com/validators?amendment=PermissionDelegation)). The project site independently says XRPK operates a validator and associated XRPL infrastructure ([XRP Kuwait](https://xrpkuwait.com/)). A mirrored public profile identifies the operation as run by `@33baid`, but the operator’s complete legal identity is not established from an accessible primary record ([TwStalker profile mirror](https://twstalker.com/XRPKuwait)).

## Official Web Presence

**Official website:** [https://xrpkuwait.com/](https://xrpkuwait.com/) — high confidence. It presents itself as XRP Kuwait/XRPK, describes the infrastructure, and is the domain cryptographically associated with the supplied validator key in a public validator registry ([Bithomp](https://bithomp.com/validators?amendment=PermissionDelegation)).

**Official X handle:** [@XRPKuwait](https://x.com/XRPKuwait) — medium-high confidence. The official XRPLF `rippled` source pairs `@Xrpkuwait` with `xrpkuwait.com` when identifying the operator of a bootstrap-server pool ([XRPLF GitHub](https://github.com/XRPLF/rippled/blob/develop/src/xrpld/overlay/detail/OverlayImpl.cpp)); an indexed profile mirror also displays the validator description and website under that handle ([TwStalker](https://twstalker.com/XRPKuwait)). The X page itself was not accessible during this research session.

## Incorporation and Operations

**Incorporation jurisdiction:** **Not established**. No accessible filing, official legal notice, company-registry record, or website disclosure establishes that XRP Kuwait/XRPK is incorporated anywhere. Confidence is high that the reviewed public evidence does not substantiate incorporation.

**Principal operating base:** **Kuwait — medium-high confidence.** The project expressly describes itself as “Built in Kuwait” and “an independent XRPL infrastructure from Kuwait” ([official site](https://xrpkuwait.com/)). This is a first-party operating-location statement, not an incorporation claim. No additional principal operating region is established.

## Activities

XRP Kuwait provides infrastructure for XRP Ledger participants. Its site identifies a validator, a pool of public hubs, stock-server services, peer-connectivity and reservation tools, a hub map, and a ledger explorer ([official site](https://xrpkuwait.com/), [servers page](https://xrpkuwait.com/servers)). The official XRPLF `rippled` code includes `hubs.xrpkuwait.com` in its default bootstrap pool and identifies that pool as operated by `@Xrpkuwait`, independently corroborating the project’s network-infrastructure role ([XRPLF GitHub](https://github.com/XRPLF/rippled/blob/develop/src/xrpld/overlay/detail/OverlayImpl.cpp)).

Its apparent stakeholders are XRP Ledger node operators, developers, and other network participants using public connectivity or informational tools. No paying-customer base, revenue model, or separate commercial product line is publicly established.

## Public Footprint

**Tier: Individual.**

**Confidence:** Moderate-low. The project maintains a dedicated website and public social presence, while the mirrored project profile describes the validator as operated by one account, `@33baid` ([TwStalker](https://twstalker.com/XRPKuwait)); the reciprocal mirrored profile associates that account with XRP Kuwait and the validator ([Instalker](https://instalker.org/33baid)). No public team roster or registered institutional structure was found. **Headcount is not established**, so the tier reflects the observable operator model rather than a verified employee count.

## Business Summary

XRP Kuwait, also styled XRPK and XRPKuwait, is a publicly identified, independently operated XRP Ledger infrastructure project rather than an established corporate or nonprofit entity. Its legal name and incorporation jurisdiction are not established. The project describes its principal operating base as Kuwait and provides XRP Ledger infrastructure: the identified mainnet validator, public hub and stock-server services, peer-connectivity and reservation tools, a hub map, and a ledger explorer. Its evident stakeholders are XRP Ledger node operators, developers, and network participants who use shared connectivity and public network information; no paying customer base or commercial model is disclosed. The website and official ecosystem references indicate network-facing reach beyond Kuwait, although no offices or incorporated operations elsewhere are established. The public footprint is classified as Individual because the project’s social presence identifies one operator and no broader team is substantiated; headcount remains unestablished.

- “XRP Kuwait,” “XRPK,” and “XRPKuwait” are supported public project stylings, but their status as registered trade names is unknown.
- The assertion that one person operates the project is derived partly from mirrored social-profile data. The operator’s legal identity and whether additional contributors exist remain unverified.
- The official X page could not be accessed directly; the handle attribution instead rests on official XRPLF source code and an indexed third-party profile mirror.
- A registrant-specific RDAP response was not accessible during this research session, so domain-registration data supplies no ownership or incorporation evidence.
- Kuwait is treated only as the self-reported principal operating base. The country-code branding, hosting details, or server geography were not used to infer incorporation.
- Headcount, revenue, customers, funding, commercial status, and operations outside Kuwait are not established.
