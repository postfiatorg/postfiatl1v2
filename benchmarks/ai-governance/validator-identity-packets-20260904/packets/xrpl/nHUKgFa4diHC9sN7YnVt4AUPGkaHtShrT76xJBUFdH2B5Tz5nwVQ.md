## Activities

Aureus Ox says it operates infrastructure for the XRP Ledger and Flare, including an XRPL validator, Flare validation, and a Flare Time Series Oracle signal provider using order-book-derived pricing. It also publishes operator-oriented blockchain analysis and offers staking information to FLR holders and delegators. [Aureus Ox homepage](https://aureusox.com/) [Official staking guide](https://aureusox.com/blog/how-to-stake-flr-on-flare/)

The LLC also develops **Oxen Flow**, described as a non-custodial mobile cryptocurrency wallet offering passkey-based access, Flare functionality, yield-related features, and XRP Ledger support. Its observable users and stakeholders include XRPL and Flare participants, FLR delegators, and mobile-wallet users. [Apple App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/nz/app/oxen-flow/id6502561067) [Oxen Flow privacy policy](https://public.oxenflow.io/legal/privacy_policy.html)

## Business Summary

Aureus Ox LLC, publicly operating as Aureus Ox, is a limited-liability blockchain-infrastructure and software company. Its incorporation jurisdiction is not established from an accessible official filing; its public company profile places its principal operating base in New York City, United States, with medium confidence. The company operates validator infrastructure for the XRP Ledger and Flare, supplies price data to Flare’s FTSO, publishes operator-oriented blockchain analysis, and develops Oxen Flow, a non-custodial wallet for Flare and XRP-related functions. Its stakeholders and users include XRPL and Flare network participants, FLR delegators, and mobile-wallet users. The services are internet-accessible and blockchain-based, but no supported office network or defined geographic market beyond the New York operating base was established. Its public institutional footprint is Micro; an exact current headcount is not established.
