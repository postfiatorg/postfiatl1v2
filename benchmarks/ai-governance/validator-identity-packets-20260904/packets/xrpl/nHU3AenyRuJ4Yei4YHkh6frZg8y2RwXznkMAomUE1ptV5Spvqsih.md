## Activities

Aesthetes operates a curated digital-art and NFT marketplace built around the XRP Ledger. Its current marketplace calls itself a creative home for XRPL NFTs and displays curated artworks and collections priced in XRP or ELS ([Aesthetes marketplace](https://aesthetes.com/)). Earlier corporate materials describe “phygital” NFTs connecting digital artworks or ownership interests with physical artworks and identify the XRP Ledger as the exchange infrastructure ([About Us](https://company.aesthetes.art/about)).

The company targets artists, collectors, curators, museums and other art institutions, offering marketplace visibility, digitalization-related opportunities and tools for blockchain-based art exchange ([Stakeholders](https://company.aesthetes.art/stakeholders)). Its relationship to the subject validator is operational: public validator data connects the supplied key to `xrpl.aesthetes.art`, and Aesthetes’ own institutional page refers to operating an XRPL validator ([key-domain record](https://mikeyexplains.com/webpage_network_health_and_validator_board.php), [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/aesthetes/?originalSubdomain=it)).

## Business Summary

Aesthetes S.r.l. is an Italian limited-liability company that publicly trades under the Aesthetes brand and is based in Milan, Lombardy. Its public-facing business is a curated digital-art marketplace built around the XRP Ledger, including NFTs and “phygital” offerings that connect digital works or rights with physical artworks. The company addresses artists, collectors, curators, museums and other art institutions, providing marketplace access, exhibition or visibility opportunities, digital-art tools and blockchain-based exchange. Its current marketplace describes itself as a creative home for XRPL NFTs, while earlier corporate materials emphasize fine-art accessibility and international collectors. Public materials also identify Aesthetes as the operator of an XRPL validator linked to the xrpl.aesthetes.art domain. The organization has a Micro public-footprint tier; a current, independently verified employee headcount is not established.
