## Activities

XLS Labs Inc. operates XPMarket as an XRP Ledger-focused software and decentralized-finance platform. Its services include token and NFT market data, portfolio tools, non-custodial interfaces for XRPL’s decentralized exchange and automated market makers, liquidity-pool tools, an NFT marketplace, token launches, farming, ETF tracking, and research content. Its stated audiences include retail users, traders, developers, token projects, liquidity providers, and the broader XRPL community. [XPMarket home page](https://xpmarket.com/) and [Terms and Conditions](https://xpmarket.com/general/terms-and-conditions).

The organization’s relationship to the validator is operational: a public validator registry maps the supplied master key to the TOML-verified `xpmarket.com` domain, while XPMarket’s own site has acknowledged using an XRPL validator node in its token-discovery process. [Bithomp validator registry](https://bithomp.com/validators?amendment=fixEnforceNFTokenTrustlineV2) and [XPMarket website](https://xpmarket.com/).

## Business Summary

XLS Labs Inc. is a Panamanian company that presents XPMarket as its public-facing digital platform. Its principal operating base is stated as Panama City, while the platform describes a global online service reach. XPMarket provides XRP Ledger market-data and analytics tools, non-custodial interfaces for native decentralized-exchange and automated-market-maker activity, portfolio and token tracking, an NFT marketplace, token-launch services, farming, and research content. Its intended users include retail participants, traders, token issuers and project teams, developers, liquidity providers, NFT participants, and the wider XRP Ledger community. ## Uncertainty

- A Panamanian registry entry, registration number, and current corporate status for XLS Labs Inc. were not independently established; incorporation rests on the company’s official legal disclosure.
- Ownership, directors, shareholders, and any parent or subsidiary relationships are not established.
- “XPMarket” is supported as the platform and public brand, but whether it is a formally registered trade name is not established.
- The validator-domain TOML file was not directly accessible during research; the domain-verification finding relies on Bithomp’s registry representation and corroborating key/domain mapping.
- Bithomp displays a United Kingdom “owner country,” while XPMarket’s legal pages state Panamanian incorporation and a Panama City base. The meaning and source of Bithomp’s country field are unclear, so it was not treated as evidence of incorporation or principal operations.
- The X page returned an access error. The handle is supported by outgoing links and organization-hosted material, not direct inspection of the X profile.
- Current headcount is not established. LinkedIn’s 2–10 range and visible employee associations are indicative rather than an audited staffing figure.
- No supported former names were found. Third-party sources differ on whether the organization began in 2021 or 2022, so no founding year is assigned.
