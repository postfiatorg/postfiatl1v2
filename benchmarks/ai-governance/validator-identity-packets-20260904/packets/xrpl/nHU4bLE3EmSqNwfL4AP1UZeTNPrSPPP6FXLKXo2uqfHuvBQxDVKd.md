# Organization Profile

## Identity

**Canonical name:** Ripple Labs Inc.  
**Entity type:** Delaware-incorporated financial-technology company. Ripple’s legal pages identify Ripple Labs Inc. and its subsidiaries collectively as “Ripple,” while recent SEC-hosted corporate documents identify Ripple Labs Inc. as a Delaware corporation. [Ripple compliance disclosures](https://ripple.com/legal/compliance/) [SEC-hosted corporate document](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2044009/000119312526347492/d114365dex22.htm)

**Supported names:** “Ripple” is the company’s public name. “Open Coin, Inc.” is a documented former legal name; no broader set of similar names is treated as an alias. [SEC complaint](https://www.sec.gov/files/litigation/complaints/2020/comp-pr2020-338.pdf)

**Validator connection:** The exact validator key `nHU4bLE3EmSqNwfL4AP1UZeTNPrSPPP6FXLKXo2uqfHuvBQxDVKd` is mapped to `ripple.com` in an XRP Ledger Foundation repository and appears in a current validator directory as “Ripple XRP Ledger Operations Team” with a domain verified through Ripple’s XRP Ledger TOML file. XRPL documentation explains that this attestation is a signed assertion that the same entity runs the validator and controls the domain serving the file. Ripple’s own privacy policy identifies `ripple.com` as a website Ripple owns or maintains. ## Activities

Ripple provides enterprise blockchain and digital-asset infrastructure. Its current offerings include cross-border payments, institutional digital-asset custody, the RLUSD stablecoin, liquidity and prime-brokerage capabilities, and corporate treasury-management software. Its customer and stakeholder groups include banks, payment providers, fintech and crypto businesses, corporate finance teams, governments, central banks, regulators, and developers. [Ripple FAQ](https://ripple.com/faq/) [Ripple custody](https://ripple.com/products/custody/) [Ripple payments](https://ripple.com/products/cross-border-payments/) [Ripple treasury management](https://ripple.com/products/treasury-management/) [Ripple company profile](https://ripple.com/company/)

Ripple also describes itself as a holder of XRP and one of multiple developers building on and contributing to the open-source XRP Ledger; it does not describe itself as the sole controller or issuer of XRP. Its connection to this validator is supported by the signed `ripple.com` domain attestation, the exact public-key mapping, and the TOML designation “Ripple XRP Ledger Operations Team.” [Ripple XRP overview](https://ripple.com/xrp/) [XRPL Foundation repository](https://github.com/XRPLF/xrpl-cli) [Bithomp validator directory](https://bithomp.com/validators?amendment=Credentials)

**Very large** — **High confidence.** Ripple reports more than 1,000 employees, at least 15 offices, operations spanning more than 90 countries, and hundreds of enterprise customers across more than 50 countries. Headcount is established only as the company’s self-reported lower bound of 1,000-plus; an exact current total is not established. [Ripple company profile](https://ripple.com/company/)

## Business Summary

Ripple Labs Inc., publicly known as Ripple, is a Delaware-incorporated financial-technology company with its principal operating base in San Francisco and offices across North America, Latin America, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. It provides enterprise blockchain and digital-asset infrastructure spanning cross-border payments, institutional custody, the RLUSD stablecoin, liquidity and prime-brokerage capabilities, and treasury-management software. Its customers and stakeholders include banks, payment providers, fintech and crypto businesses, corporate treasury teams, governments, central banks, regulators, and developers. Ripple also develops and contributes to the open-source XRP Ledger ecosystem and is institutionally linked to the profiled mainnet validator through the ripple.com domain attestation and public-key mapping. Ripple reports operations in more than 90 countries, at least 15 offices, and more than 1,000 employees, supporting a Very large public-footprint tier.

## Uncertainty

The public validator records establish a high-confidence connection to the Ripple organization, but they do not identify which specific Ripple Labs Inc. subsidiary legally owns or administers the server. “Ripple XRP Ledger Operations Team” appears as the declared operational principal, not as a separately established legal entity. The validator’s physical hosting location and personnel are intentionally not inferred. Ripple’s exact current headcount is also not established beyond its self-reported figure of more than 1,000 employees. “Open Coin, Inc.” is retained only as a documented former legal name; unsupported spelling variants and similarly named entities are excluded.
