# Organization Profile

## Identity

The canonical public name is **Vet**. The evidence supports a **pseudonymous individual operator**, not a named company or other incorporated organization. Vet’s legal name and any legal entity behind the persona are not established. No supported aliases or former names were found.

The operator’s website states, in the first person, “I’m Vet” and calls `xrp.vet` the domain associated with “my XRPL Validator”; its validator link targets the supplied public key. [XRPSCAN associates that exact key with `xrp.vet`](https://classic.xrpscan.com/validator/nHBWa56Vr7csoFcCnEPzCCKVvnDQw3L28mATgHYQMGtbEfUjuYyB), while [Bithomp identifies the verified-domain operator as “Vet” with handle `VET_X0`](https://bithomp.com/validators?amendment=Credentials). XRPL documentation explains that completed two-way domain verification is strong evidence that the same entity controls the domain and validator key. [Official domain-verification documentation](https://xrpl.org/docs/infrastructure/configuration/server-modes/run-xrpld-as-a-validator)

## Official Web Presence

- **Official website:** [https://xrp.vet/](https://xrp.vet/). It expressly identifies itself as the domain associated with Vet’s validator.
- **Official X handle:** [@Vet_X0](https://x.com/Vet_X0). The validator website links directly to this account, and the account identifies Vet as an XRP Ledger validator and links back to `xrp.vet`. [Indexed X profile](https://x.com/Vet_X0/highlights)

## Incorporation and Operations

- **Incorporation jurisdiction:** **Not established** — high confidence. Neither the [official website](https://xrp.vet/) nor the [linked X profile](https://x.com/Vet_X0) identifies an incorporated entity, legal name, registration number, or filing jurisdiction.
- **Principal operating regions:** **Not established** — high confidence. The official X profile gives “Backyard,” not a verifiable geographic location. Bithomp displays Germany as an owner-country metadata value, but XRPL documentation defines that field as the owner’s self-declared main legal jurisdiction; it does not independently establish incorporation or a principal operating base. [Bithomp record](https://bithomp.com/validators?amendment=Credentials); [field definition](https://xrpl.org/docs/references/xrp-ledger-toml)

## Activities

Vet’s directly supported principal activity is operating the XRP Ledger validator associated with `xrp.vet` and participating publicly in the XRP Ledger community. The [official X profile](https://x.com/Vet_X0/highlights) describes the account as a validator and community contributor and contains technical and educational discussions about the ledger.

A public biography under the matching `Vet_X0` persona says Vet launched the validator in 2022, co-founded `xrp.cafe`, produced XRPL educational content, hosted community discussions, contributed protocol proposals, operated NFT token-gating tools, and launched an RLUSD-tracking tool. These are self-reported activities rather than evidence that `xrp.cafe` or another company legally operates the validator. [Vet public biography](https://xpert.page/vet_x0) The observable stakeholder audience is the XRP Ledger community—including developers, projects, creators, and XRP users. A defined customer base for the validator operation is not established.

## Public Footprint

**Individual.** Confidence: **high**. The official site consistently uses singular first-person language—“I’m Vet” and “my XRPL Validator”—and the matching biography presents the work as that of one person. [Official site](https://xrp.vet/); [public biography](https://xpert.page/vet_x0)

Headcount is **not established**. The [indexed X profile](https://x.com/Vet_X0/highlights) shows a substantial public audience, but social reach does not demonstrate employees or a larger institution.

## Business Summary

Vet is the public pseudonym of an individual XRP Ledger participant who operates the mainnet validator associated with xrp.vet. No legal name, incorporated entity, incorporation jurisdiction, or principal operating base is established in the public sources reviewed. The operator's documented activities center on maintaining the validator and participating in the XRP Ledger ecosystem through technical and educational content, public discussions, protocol proposals, and tools. A matching public profile also describes co-founding xrp.cafe, but no evidence establishes that business as the validator's legal operator. The observable stakeholder audience is the XRP Ledger community, including developers, projects, creators, and XRP users; no defined paying customer group is established. Activity is internet-based and reaches an international audience, while the operator's physical base remains unknown. Public footprint tier: Individual; headcount is not established.
