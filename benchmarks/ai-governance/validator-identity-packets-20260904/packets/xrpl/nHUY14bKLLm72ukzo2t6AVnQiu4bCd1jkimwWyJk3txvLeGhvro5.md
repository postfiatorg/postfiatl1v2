## Activities

GateHub provides web and mobile digital-asset services centered on XRP Ledger and Xahau accounts. Its offering includes non-custodial XRPL and Xahau wallets, hosted wallets, interfaces for decentralized and hosted trading, transfers, payments, wallet and portfolio analytics, and gateways for supported fiat and crypto deposits and withdrawals. Customers include individual and company account holders and other digital-asset users requiring wallet, exchange-interface, payment, or gateway functionality. ## Business Summary

GateHub Limited is a UK-incorporated private limited company that uses GateHub as its public brand and anchors a group with operating subsidiaries in Ljubljana, Slovenia. The group provides an online digital-asset platform centered on XRP Ledger and Xahau wallets, hosted wallets, decentralized-exchange and hosted-exchange interfaces, asset transfers, fiat and crypto deposit and withdrawal routes, payments, market tools, and portfolio analytics. Its customers and stakeholders are individual and company account holders, digital-asset users, gateway customers, and participants in the XRP Ledger ecosystem. Its principal operating base appears to be Ljubljana, while its legal parent is registered in England and Wales; its services have cross-border online reach subject to jurisdictional restrictions. Its public footprint is Small, supported by 25 employees reported for the Slovenian development subsidiary at year-end 2024, although group-wide headcount is not established.
