## Activities

Cabbit provides high-speed, high-availability networking and server infrastructure, including routing capacity stated as reaching 100 Gbps and connectivity through relationships with Tier-1 internet service providers ([official website](https://cabbit.tech/)). Its customers are described only as diverse clients worldwide; specific customers are not publicly established.

The company also states that it operates an XRP Ledger validator as a service to that ecosystem. The direct link is strong: Cabbit’s site publishes `nHUcNC5ni7XjVYfCMe38Rm3KQaq27jw7wJpcUYdo4miWwpNePRTw` as its public XRPL validation key ([Cabbit Technology](https://cabbit.tech/)).
