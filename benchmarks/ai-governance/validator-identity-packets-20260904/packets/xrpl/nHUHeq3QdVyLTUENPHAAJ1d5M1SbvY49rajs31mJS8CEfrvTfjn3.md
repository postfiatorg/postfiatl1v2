# Organization Profile

## Identity

**Canonical public name:** Squid.  
**Current contracting legal entity:** Ceiba Network Inc.  
**Entity type:** Privately operated cross-chain blockchain infrastructure company/platform. Squid’s official materials describe a unified team, products, revenue model, and commercial integrations, while its current terms identify Ceiba Network Inc. as the contracting party for the services. [Squid’s official profile](https://www.squidrouter.com/blog/state-of-squid) [Terms of Service](https://www.squidrouter.com/terms)

**Supported aliases or former names:** Not established. “Squid Router” is reflected in the domain and social handle but is not established as a separate formal alias.

**Validator connection:** The explorer record for public key `nHUHeq3QdVyLTUENPHAAJ1d5M1SbvY49rajs31mJS8CEfrvTfjn3` identifies `squidrouter.com` as its TOML-verified domain and labels it “Fig.” Separately, Squid states on its official site that it runs an XRPL UNL validator. Together, these provide strong evidence that the validator belongs to the Squid organization, although the TOML file was not directly accessed during this research. ## Incorporation and Operations

**Incorporation jurisdiction:** **Panama — medium confidence.** Squid’s current terms name Ceiba Network Inc. as the contracting entity, apply the substantive laws of the Republic of Panama, and specify Panama City arbitration. Its privacy notice describes Ceiba as an establishment operating under applicable Panamanian data-protection law. No independent registry extract confirming Ceiba’s incorporation date or registration number was located. [Terms of Service](https://www.squidrouter.com/terms) [Privacy Policy](https://www.squidrouter.com/privacy)

**Principal operating regions:** **Globally distributed and fully remote — high confidence.** Squid states that its roughly twenty-person team is globally distributed and fully remote. A single principal physical operating base is not established; Panama is the indicated legal jurisdiction, not necessarily the team’s operational headquarters. [State of Squid](https://www.squidrouter.com/blog/state-of-squid)

## Activities

Squid develops cross-chain digital-asset infrastructure and consumer products. Its stated stack comprises a settlement protocol, routing and aggregation engine, frontend application, REST API, SDK, widget, hooks, and bespoke integrations. These services let users and integrators swap, bridge, send, and route assets across more than 100 blockchain networks and numerous liquidity sources. [Official product description](https://www.squidrouter.com/) [State of Squid](https://www.squidrouter.com/blog/state-of-squid)

Its customers and stakeholders include retail users, developers, wallets, payment platforms, neobanks, decentralized-finance applications, asset issuers, institutions, and blockchain ecosystems. Squid reports more than 1,000 application integrations. [Official website](https://www.squidrouter.com/)

Squid describes its XRPL role as broader than asset routing: it says it applied to participate in XRPL governance, runs an UNL validator, and provides cross-chain XRP and XRPL integrations. [State of Squid](https://www.squidrouter.com/blog/state-of-squid)

## Public Footprint

Its wider institutional footprint includes more than 1,000 reported integrations, over one million users, and support for more than 100 blockchain networks. **Confidence: high** for the tier based on the organization’s direct team-size disclosure. [Team disclosure](https://www.squidrouter.com/blog/state-of-squid) [About Squid](https://www.squidrouter.com/about)

## Business Summary

Squid is the public brand of a privately operated cross-chain blockchain infrastructure business whose current service terms identify Ceiba Network Inc. as the contracting entity. The legal materials point to Panama, while no single principal physical operating base is established; the approximately twenty-person team describes itself as fully remote and globally distributed. Squid provides a consumer application and infrastructure—including a routing engine, settlement protocol, APIs, SDKs, widgets, and bespoke integrations—for swapping, bridging, sending, and embedding digital-asset transfers across blockchain networks. Its customers and stakeholders include retail users, developers, wallets, payment platforms, decentralized-finance applications, asset issuers, institutions, and blockchain ecosystems. Its products span more than 100 chains and are used through its own application and partner integrations. The organization falls in the Small size tier based on its stated approximate team size.

## Uncertainty

- The public brand **Squid** is clearly established, but the precise ownership or corporate relationship between the Squid team, brand, validator, and Ceiba Network Inc. is not publicly explained.
- Panama is supported as Ceiba’s legal jurisdiction by Squid’s current legal pages, but incorporation was not independently confirmed through an accessible official registry extract.
- No single operational headquarters is established. A globally distributed, fully remote team is supported; server, registrar, and validator infrastructure locations were excluded from the location analysis.
- The exact validator-domain link relies on Bithomp’s report of TOML verification because the underlying `xrp-ledger.toml` file was not directly accessible during research. Squid’s independent statement that it runs an XRPL validator corroborates the organizational connection.
- The validator label “Fig” corresponds to the public name of a Squid co-founder, but this does not establish that this individual personally owns or exclusively controls the validator.
- No formal aliases or former organizational names were established.
