## Business Summary

University of North Carolina Kenan-Flagler Business School is a business school and academic unit of the public University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. It is not shown to be separately incorporated; its parent university system is a North Carolina body politic and corporate. Based principally in Chapel Hill, with teaching in Charlotte and online delivery, the School provides undergraduate, MBA, accounting, management, doctoral, and executive education, conducts applied business research, and engages employers and organizational clients. Its stakeholders include students, alumni, faculty, employers, companies, nonprofits, and government bodies in North Carolina, across the United States, and internationally. Its public institutional footprint is classified Large, while a verified payroll headcount is not established. Public validator data links the supplied key to a School-hosted UNC subdomain, but the responsible internal unit or personnel are undisclosed.
