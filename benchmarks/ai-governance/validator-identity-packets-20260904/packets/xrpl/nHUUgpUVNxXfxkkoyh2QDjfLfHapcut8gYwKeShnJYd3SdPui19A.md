# Organization Profile

## Identity

The operator is identified with **medium-high confidence** as **Peersyst Technology S.L.**, a Spanish limited-liability software company. The public-facing names **Peersyst** and **Peersyst Technology** are supported by the [Catalonia Startup Hub](https://startupshub.catalonia.com/startup/barcelona/peersyst/4904), the company’s [official website](https://peersyst.com/), and its [official GitHub organization](https://github.com/Peersyst). No former name is established.

The connection to the validator is evidence-based: Bithomp associates the exact public key `nHUUgpUVNxXfxkkoyh2QDjfLfHapcut8gYwKeShnJYd3SdPui19A` with “Peersyst Technology” and the cryptographically verified domain `peersyst.cloud` on its [validator registry](https://bithomp.com/validators?amendment=Credentials). XRPL documentation explains that validator domain verification creates a two-way, signed association between a validator key and the domain serving its TOML file, providing strong evidence of common control ([XRPL domain-verification specification](https://xrpl.org/docs/references/xrp-ledger-toml)).

## Incorporation and Operations

- **Incorporation jurisdiction — Spain; confidence: High.** Spain’s Official Gazette records the constitution of **Peersyst Technology S.L.** in 2019 and its registration in the Barcelona Mercantile Registry ([2019 BORME record](https://www.boe.es/diario_borme/txt.php?id=BORME-A-2019-215-08)). A later official filing records its Barcelona registered-office change in 2022 ([2022 BORME record](https://www.boe.es/diario_borme/txt.php?id=BORME-A-2022-207-08)).

- **Principal operating base — Barcelona, Spain; confidence: High.** Barcelona is identified by the company’s [official company page](https://peersyst.com/company), its [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Peersyst), and the [Catalonia Startup Hub](https://startupshub.catalonia.com/startup/barcelona/peersyst/4904).

- **Additional operating location — Andorra; confidence: Moderate.** Peersyst’s official company page lists both Barcelona and Andorra as locations, but an Andorran legal entity or registered establishment was not established ([official company page](https://peersyst.com/company)).

- **Project reach — international; confidence: Moderate.** The company reports projects from more than 20 countries and documents work involving institutions in Europe and Latin America ([official company page](https://peersyst.com/company); [official case studies](https://peersyst.com/case-studies/)).

## Activities

Peersyst develops and consults on blockchain software. Its stated activities include protocol-level development, sidechains and bridges, central-bank digital-currency pilots, digital wallets and payments, asset-tokenization systems, and private blockchain networks. Its customers and stakeholders include blockchain protocols and developer communities, governments, central and commercial banks, enterprises, and wallet users ([official website](https://peersyst.com/); [official company page](https://peersyst.com/company); [official case studies](https://peersyst.com/case-studies/)).

Its relationship to the specified validator is that of the identified organizational operator: the exact key is associated with “Peersyst Technology” and `peersyst.cloud` through the validator’s verified-domain record ([Bithomp validator registry](https://bithomp.com/validators?amendment=Credentials)). This establishes the domain-to-key connection; the broader public-source chain links the Peersyst Technology name to Peersyst Technology S.L.

## Business Summary

Peersyst Technology S.L., publicly branded as Peersyst, is a Spanish limited-liability software company incorporated and registered in Barcelona. Its principal operating base is Barcelona, with an additional stated location in Andorra and projects spanning multiple countries. The company develops and consults on blockchain infrastructure and applications, including protocol-level software, sidechains and bridges, central-bank digital-currency pilots, digital wallets and payments, asset-tokenization systems, and private networks. Its customer and stakeholder groups include blockchain protocols and their developer communities, governments, central banks, commercial banks, enterprises, and end users of wallet products. Peersyst is linked to the specified XRP Ledger validator through the validator key’s cryptographically verified peersyst.cloud domain association and public registry naming. Its public footprint is Micro: a corporate LinkedIn page reports a 2–10 employee range, while its official web and GitHub presence show an internationally active project portfolio. Exact current headcount is not established.

## Uncertainty

- The `peersyst.cloud` TOML file could not be retrieved directly during this research. The domain-key link therefore relies on Bithomp’s verified-domain result and the XRPL specification’s explanation of that verification, rather than direct inspection of the TOML contents.
- Public sources establish the Peersyst Technology name and strongly connect it to Peersyst Technology S.L., but the validator metadata does not expose a corporate registration number.
- An Andorran office or operating presence is stated by Peersyst, but an Andorran incorporation, branch registration, or separate legal entity was not established.
- Apple application listings name Peersyst Technology S.L. as seller but use “Peersyst Technology Global SL” in a copyright line ([Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/near-mobile-crypto-wallet/id6443501225)). No corroborating registry record or validator-domain link for that latter name was found, so it is not treated as an alias or the validator operator.
- Exact current headcount, ownership, group structure, and the legal owner of `peersyst.cloud` remain unestablished.
