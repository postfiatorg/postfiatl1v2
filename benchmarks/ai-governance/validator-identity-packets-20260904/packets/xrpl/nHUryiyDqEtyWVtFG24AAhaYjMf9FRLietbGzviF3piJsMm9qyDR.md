**Incorporation jurisdiction:** Costa Rica — **high confidence**. The current terms identify the operator as a Costa Rican limited-liability company. Its LEI record gives Costa Rica as its legal jurisdiction, National Register of Costa Rica identifier `3-102-931484`, and Garabito, Puntarenas as its registered headquarters locality. ([Bitrue Terms of Use](https://support.bitrue.com/hc/en-001/articles/4405473110681-Bitrue-Terms-of-Use), [Bloomberg LEI record](https://lei.bloomberg.com/leis/view/984500E7983FB2A8AF49))

No single principal operational headquarters is established publicly; the Costa Rican LEI headquarters entry should not, by itself, be treated as proof that day-to-day platform operations are centered there. ## Activities

Bitrue operates a digital-asset platform serving cryptocurrency traders and token projects. Its official site offers cryptocurrency purchases, spot and perpetual-futures trading, leveraged products, copy trading, staking and other yield products, crypto-backed loans, listings, and API access. ([Bitrue platform](https://www.bitrue.com/en))

Its relationship to this validator is twofold: Bitrue publicly says that it runs an XRP validator, while the validator registry associates the specified key with Bitrue’s official domain. ([Bitrue validator statement](https://support.bitrue.com/hc/en-001/articles/360051010434-Looking-Back-On-2-Years-Of-Success), [Bithomp validator registry](https://bithomp.com/validators))

## Business Summary

Bitrue is the public-facing name of STRALINK INNOVATIONS TECHNOLOGIES LIMITADA, a Costa Rican limited liability company that operates the Bitrue digital-asset platform. Its incorporation and registered headquarters jurisdiction are Costa Rica; official materials describe a distributed operating presence across Europe and Asia, but do not establish a single principal operational headquarters. The platform serves retail and professional cryptocurrency traders and token projects through digital-asset buying and selling, spot and derivatives trading, copy trading, staking and yield products, collateralized cryptocurrency loans, and API access. Its reach is international, subject to stated jurisdictional exclusions, and the company reports tens of millions of users across many countries. The organization is classified as Very large based on its public platform footprint and cross-regional operations, although employee headcount is not established. Public validator records connect the specified XRP Ledger validator key to Bitrue’s official domain.

## Uncertainty

- The domain-hosted validator TOML and its cryptographic attestation were not independently inspected. The key attribution therefore rests on the public registry mapping, XRPL’s documented domain-association mechanism, Bitrue’s control of the mapped domain, and Bitrue’s separate statement that it operates an XRP validator.
- The current legal operator’s LEI record gives a creation date in April 2025, while Bitrue says the platform launched in 2018. The identity of any predecessor operating entity—and whether it previously controlled this validator—is not established. ([Bloomberg LEI record](https://lei.bloomberg.com/leis/view/984500E7983FB2A8AF49), [Bitrue history](https://support.bitrue.com/hc/en-001/articles/360051010434-Looking-Back-On-2-Years-Of-Success))
- No single principal operational headquarters and no current employee headcount were established.
- “Bitrue Lithuania UAB,” referenced in a separate Bitrue announcement, and the “Bitrue Inc.” wording appearing in some product copyright notices were not established as aliases, former names, or operators of this validator and are therefore excluded from the canonical identity. ([Bitrue Lithuania announcement](https://www.bitrue.com/blog/Bitrue-Secures-VASP-Registration-in-Lithuania))
- An older futures agreement selected Seychelles law, but Seychelles’ regulator subsequently stated that the current Costa Rican operator has no legal or operational nexus there. Seychelles was therefore not treated as an incorporation or operating jurisdiction. ([Older futures agreement](https://www.bitrue.com/static/futures-agreement/BitrueFuturesServiceAgreement-en_US.pdf), [Seychelles FSA notice](https://fsaseychelles.sc/media-corner/regulatory-updates/warning-unauthorised-activity-under-the-virtual-asset-service-providers-act-2045))
