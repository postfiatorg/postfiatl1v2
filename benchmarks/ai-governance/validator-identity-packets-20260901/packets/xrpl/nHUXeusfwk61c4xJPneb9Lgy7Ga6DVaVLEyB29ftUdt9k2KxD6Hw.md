## Business Summary

The Integrators B.V. is a Dutch private limited liability company trading as XRPL Labs and operating under the Xaman brand. Incorporated in the Netherlands and publicly based in Amersfoort, it develops open-source and commercial software and network infrastructure for the XRP Ledger and Xahau. Its principal offering is Xaman, a self-custodial mobile wallet supporting account creation, signing, payments, token exchange, embedded xApps, subscription services, and hardware-card integrations. The company serves individual wallet users, software developers, ecosystem partners, and other participants in a geographically distributed blockchain community. Public materials also describe operation of XRPL protocol-chain infrastructure, including a validator role. Its public institutional footprint is small, with an official roster naming approximately eleven team members, although current legal headcount is not established. The business maintains a Netherlands operating base while distributing software and services internationally.

- Bithomp currently displays a TOML-based “Verified domain” label for `validator.xrpl-labs.com`, but the underlying network-specific attestation was not independently retrieved or cryptographically checked here. The machine-readable value therefore remains `null`.
- Bithomp presents Wietse Wind as the validator owner, while official corporate terms say The Integrators B.V. operates dUNL validator infrastructure. Public evidence does not resolve whether this specific master key is legally or technically controlled by the individual, the company, or under another operational arrangement.

- Current legal headcount is not established. The official team page provides a public roster, not a complete employment filing.
- An older official privacy notice placed the company in Amsterdam-Duivendrecht, whereas the current privacy statement and European Commission directory place it in Amersfoort. This appears consistent with an office relocation, but the move was not independently verified through the Dutch registry.
