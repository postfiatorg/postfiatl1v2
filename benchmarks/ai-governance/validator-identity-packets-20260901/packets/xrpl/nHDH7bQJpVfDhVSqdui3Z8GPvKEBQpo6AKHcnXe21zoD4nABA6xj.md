## Business Summary

University of Waterloo is an Ontario statutory body corporate and research university based in Waterloo, Ontario, Canada. Established under provincial legislation, it provides undergraduate and graduate education, co-operative and work-integrated learning, research, entrepreneurship support, and related academic services. Its principal stakeholders include students, faculty, staff, research partners, employers, governments, nonprofit organizations, donors, and alumni. Operations are centered on its Waterloo main campus and satellite campuses in Kitchener, Cambridge, and Stratford, with international reach through research collaborations, exchange programs, co-operative employers, and an alumni network. Official figures report more than 41,000 students and, for 2022, 1,378 faculty members and 2,811 staff, placing it in the very-large institutional tier.
