## Business Summary

The University of North Carolina Kenan-Flagler Business School is a public-university business school and academic unit of the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, within the North Carolina, United States, public university system. Based principally in Chapel Hill, with a Charlotte campus and online delivery, it provides undergraduate business, management, accounting, MBA, doctoral and executive-development education, alongside faculty research and public-service activity.
