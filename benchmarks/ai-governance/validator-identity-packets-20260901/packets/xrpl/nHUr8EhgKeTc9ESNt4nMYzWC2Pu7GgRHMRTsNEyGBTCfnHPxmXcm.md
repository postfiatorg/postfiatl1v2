## Business Summary

Anodos Labs Inc. is a privately held financial-technology corporation incorporated in Wyoming, United States, with a remote operating footprint reported across Europe and Canada and a visible technical presence in Thessaloniki, Greece. Founded in 2023 and converted to a Wyoming corporation in 2025, the company develops blockchain-based, non-custodial financial software and infrastructure centered on the XRP Ledger. Its public product portfolio includes the ANODEX decentralized-exchange interface, wallet and onboarding tools, and a planned neobank-style financial application supporting transfers, asset exchange, cards, and related services through blockchain protocols and licensed partners where applicable. The company addresses individual users, businesses, developers, and XRP Ledger ecosystem participants and describes its reach as international. Available public materials indicate a small, remote organization, although exact employee headcount is not established.
