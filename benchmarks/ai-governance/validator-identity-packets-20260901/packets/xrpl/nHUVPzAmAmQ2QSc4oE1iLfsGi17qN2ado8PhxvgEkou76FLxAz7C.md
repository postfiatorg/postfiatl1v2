## Business Summary

University of Kansas Institute for Information Sciences (I2S) is a public-university research institute and an organizational unit of the University of Kansas, not a separately incorporated business. Its parent is a Kansas state agency, and the institute’s principal operating base is Lawrence, Kansas. I2S conducts research, teaching, service, technology development and commercialization across computer systems, communications and networking, radar and remote sensing, cybersecurity, mathematical computing, cyber-social dynamics and blockchain. Its stakeholders include students, faculty researchers, public research sponsors, government collaborators, industry partners and regional and national technology users. Activities are centered in Kansas with regional and national reach. Public sources indicate more than 40 faculty and staff researchers, about 140 students, 85 grants and more than $21 million in grant funding over five years, supporting a Medium public-profile tier. Public registry evidence associates its legacy ITTC domain with the identified XRP Ledger validator, but current cryptographic control of the key was not independently established.
