Why proof reuse fails, verified beyond source inspection:

- The original public witness passes native Nitro/statement verification.
- Changing only `manifest.nav_asset_id` fails `manifest differs from measured account policy`.
- Changing the measured policy and recomputing its source commitment as well fails `manifest digest mismatch` against the signed reading.
- The signed reading commits the manifest, the exact reserve public values, its ephemeral statement signature and the Nitro attestation binding. Re-proving the old reading with a different asset is therefore insufficient.

There are three additional ordering/sizing constraints in the unchanged code:

1. Finalize a packet declaring the intended supply `S`, then mint **all S to one opening inventory holder**, then initialize the route with that exact opening inventory. Creating the route after a nonzero-supply packet but before minting fails the issued-supply/inventory equality. The proposed route-before-mint sequence is not the valid opening path. A zero-supply packet/route followed by a funded primary subscription is a different path.
2. Route initialization must use `live_value_enabled=false`. The appointed issuer/reserve operator subsequently signs `pftl_uniswap_route_epoch_advance`, increasing route/policy epochs and enabling live value after configuration checks. This is ordinary authorized route administration, not a consensus upgrade or validator-governance amendment.
3. An unreserved opening export must export **the complete opening inventory**. A 5,000-token slice of approximately 419,000 initial tokens fails `pftl_uniswap_export_entitlement_required`. To export exactly 5,000 as opening inventory, choose `S=5,000`, or use a properly funded reservation/subscription path. Release 4 enforces `NAV=floor(verified_net_assets*1e6/S_atoms)`: using the full $420,244.15 with only 5,000 tokens makes NAV **$84.048830**, not $1.002487. Approximately $5,012.435 of separately attributable backing would support 5,000 at $1.002487. That is a sizing example, not an attested balance.

### C. Founder decisions, execution gates and estimate

The remaining founder inputs/actions are:

1. **Backing and old B23:** choose retirement/migration of existing obligations or a separately funded Schwab account. Approve the exact new asset name/code, opening supply, target opening NAV and which holder receives it. Merely agreeing to a new coin does not authorize double-counting the old reserve. Account-specific policy/PCR measurement and fresh reading are required.
2. **Appoint PFTL roles:** issuer, reserve submitter/operator, redemption account, opening inventory holder, relayer and Ethereum-checkpoint authority/operator. Fund the necessary native transaction fees and holder trustline reserves. Keep signing in the owners' systems. Appoint the operational owner for daily readings, stale-NAV handling and recovery; prepared daily automation is not an enabled service.
3. **Appoint Ethereum roles:** deployment signer with ETH, wrapped-token owner for one-time controller lock, controller governance/pause authority, LP position owner, initial mint recipient and optional test buyer. The mainnet token/controller/verifier addresses and runtime hashes must be fixed and reviewed together before the native route is pinned.
4. **Approve policy and return choice:** 10050/9995 with legacy field omission is the existing-guest path. Approve appropriate native caps and the immutable Ethereum caps, or commission lower-cap contracts. Decide whether exact release 4 is a firm limit; the hardened V3 return requires a separate consensus upgrade and validator rollout. No unsafe legacy-return acceptance was inferred here.
5. **Fund the launch:** 2,000 real USDC for LP liquidity, 5,000 correctly backed native/wrapped units if that sizing is selected, and ETH for deployment/proof/LP transactions. A real 2,000-USDC buyer test needs another 2,000 USDC in the buyer's wallet. Schwab backing, LP cash and optional buyer cash are separate amounts. Cash redemption also needs independently tracked settlement liquidity; a Schwab NAV proof does not create PFUSDC reserves. Refresh gas at signing: Phase 1's deployment estimate included only a provisional proof allowance and is not a current funded quote.
6. **Authorize the actual chain sequence only after qualification:** fresh capture/proof → create/register/finalize → mint complete opening inventory → deploy Ethereum contracts paused and verify runtime/roles → initialize fresh native route disabled → issuer-signed epoch advance/enable → export exactly the supported inventory/reserved amount → obtain genuine finalized archive witness and receipt proof → accept receipt/mint once → record destination consumption → seed LP → optional bounded buyer swap. Before offering burn, qualify the selected return contract, checkpoint signer, receipt/finality proofs and native credit end to end twice. Only then approve mainnet go, exact unsigned transactions and monitoring/rollback procedures. Controller pause does not reverse burns, remove LP inventory or stop token transfers.

Remaining material risks: overlapping reserve claims; whole-account attested valuation rather than segregated legal ownership or cryptographic brokerage solvency; stale reading windows and operating downtime; legacy route inventory behavior; wide immutable caps; old-versus-new guest/profile/version mix-ups; checkpoint/committee anchor and rotation correctness; BFT return-authority availability/trust; V3 event incompatibility; receipt/relay failures after irreversible burn; packet deadlines/refund races; finite USDC liquidity and slippage; broker/business-day funding and withdrawal delays. No safe round-trip, production rollout or mainnet-readiness claim follows from the component proofs.
