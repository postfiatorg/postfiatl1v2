## Faults found, and what was done

1. **The host reviewer refused every real settlement.** `authorize` required `paused` to be `false`; the node omits the field when it is false. Fixed with a test in `270b9eed3` and installed on six hosts.
2. **On validators 1, 2, 3 and 5, `/etc/postfiat` and `/opt/postfiat/releases` belonged to the node user.** The reviewer rightly refuses to sign there. Both are now `root:root 755`. The previous owner and mode are saved in `/root/etc-postfiat-owner.before` and `/root/opt-releases-owner.before` on each host. Nothing has needed write access since, but the deploy scripts should be checked.
3. **`exit_govern.py paid` only settles the canary.** `review_paid` pins the b23-bob owner and destination. User withdrawals are settled by `ops/sol-live/oncall/settle.py`: it checks the payout the same way for any holder and amount, then calls `govern` with the chain's rules. Each host's own reviewer still decides whether to sign.
4. **The public Solana endpoint answered the relay host in 5 to 10 seconds** during the evening of 24 November. The deposit screen showed "Deposits are paused" twice. Two changes are running:
   - the health watcher asks a second provider before closing deposits ([PR 229](https://github.com/postfiatorg/postfiatl1v2/pull/229));
   - the Task Node Solana client takes a list of providers and reads the depositor's standard USDC account by its derived address, without the indexed call ([Task Node PR 436](https://github.com/postfiatorg/tasknode/pull/436)). The relay's `L1_SOLANA_RPC_URL` is now `publicnode, mainnet-beta`.

   Both providers are free public endpoints. A paid provider is the real fix.
5. **The payout service self-test fails** with "Wrong role-proof binding". The migration changed the config, and the possession record of 23 November is bound to the old one. Payouts are not affected. The record has to be signed again with the fee and pause keys.
6. **The rollout guard needed 29 blocks of margin**, so the height moved from 1880 to 1890. `exit_roll.py` and `exit_release_kit.py` had the predecessor release hard-coded; they now read `EXIT_OLD_RELEASE` and `EXIT_OLDER_RELEASE`.
7. **The reviewer installer** left its virtual environment unreadable under umask 077, and the hosts lacked `acl` and `python3.12-venv`. Fixed in the branch; the packages were installed by hand.
8. **The bundle list did not name `exit_rules.py`**, which the service imports. Fixed in the branch.
9. **Vast credit ran out again** at about 15:00 UTC on 24 November. The old prover 61487772 had started itself and was billing $3.84 an hour. It is stopped. The credit alert (PR 213) is still not installed.

## Limits that hold today

- **One withdrawal spends one batch.** A wallet with several batches sees each as its own option; the largest batch is the largest single withdrawal.
- **Withdrawals below 0.30 USDC are refused.** Balances under that in a batch cannot leave. Five of the nine batches hold less than 0.30 each.
- **The vault accepts 25 USDC more.** Raising the cap is the owner's decision.
- **A withdrawal takes about four minutes.** The proof is about two of them.
- **Cancel is refused while the route is paused**, as designed in PR 223.
