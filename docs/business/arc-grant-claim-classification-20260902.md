# pfUSDC on Arc proposal v3: claim-classification packet for independent audit review

This packet classifies the material claims in [grant proposal v3 at its publication commit](https://github.com/postfiatorg/postfiatl1v2/blob/3f393b76ef0b55f52273caa7d5159660b31e888d/docs/business/pfusdc-arc-grant-proposal-20260828-v3.md). The current-source baseline is the pre-packet branch head [\`85cb3c91\`](https://github.com/postfiatorg/postfiatl1v2/commit/85cb3c9183ce5998e3e9a20a87106fadf7e1f8af). Historical evidence is labeled historical and does not qualify the current source or the current PFTL devnet.

Do not claim review, audit, endorsement, or co-sponsorship until the auditor supplies it. This packet is for the auditor's review; it is not evidence of review, audit, endorsement, or co-sponsorship by the auditor.

In this five-class taxonomy, DEMONSTRATED ON CONTROLLED DEVNET means receipt-backed, non-production execution evidence; each row names whether the execution used controlled PFTL, Arc testnet, or Arbitrum Sepolia. VERIFIED IN REPO means the cited source, fixture, artifact, or test is present and reproducible from its named commit; it does not mean independently audited.
