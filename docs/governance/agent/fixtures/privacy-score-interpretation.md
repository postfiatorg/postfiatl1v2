# Interpreting the privacy fixture scores

The historical privacy deanonymization, correlation, longitudinal linkage, floor calibration and observer inference fixtures record candidate counts and equal-weight estimates. For a declared observer with k equally likely candidates, the single-guess estimate is 1/k. Preserve the signed fixture bytes when verifying their attestation hashes. Reading a field named posterior or maximum in those fixtures requires the equal-weight assumption.

With individual conditional probabilities, the best guess gets the largest probability. The worked fixture includes a 16-candidate case with a 40% best guess. [Distribution examples](../../../fixtures/privacy/candidate-weights.json)
